# Slack thread capture

A brain reaction creates a canonical snapshot containing ordered messages, speakers, timestamps,
message permalinks, and attachment boundaries. After acquisition, that snapshot and its attachments
enter the same capture envelope, extraction, source renderer, librarian, gates, and writer used by
the local bridge and backoffice.
