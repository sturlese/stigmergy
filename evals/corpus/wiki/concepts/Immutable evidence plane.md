# Immutable evidence plane

Exact submitted bytes live in the private object store under content-addressed SHA-256 keys. Each
capture also produces one readable, immutable source page in Git. The object preserves binary
fidelity, the source preserves inspectable evidence and citations, and ordinary notes and concepts
hold the conclusions worth retrieving.
