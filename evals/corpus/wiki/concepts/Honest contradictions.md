# Honest contradictions

When credible sources disagree, the wiki retains both dated claims and citations in a structured
contradiction marker at the narrowest safe visibility. That uncertainty is healthy knowledge. A
master may later submit a supported resolution as a new capture, but the original write never
blocks for an answer.
