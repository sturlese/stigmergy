- `corpus/`: canonical note, concept, source, entity, and ACL fixture.
- `retrieval_golden.json` and `run_retrieval.py`: hybrid ranking measurement.
- `qa_golden.json`, `qa_identities.json`, and `run_qa.py`: grounded answer measurement.
- `run_gates.py` and `bars.py`: release thresholds.
- `eval_history.py` and `history.ndjson`: append-only results from real runs.
