Halcyon Grid pilot conversation

Sat down with the people running the Halcyon Grid pilot to understand what they actually need
from us before the next phase starts.

Halcyon Grid is running a small number of sites on their own scheduling stack and wants to know
whether ours can take over the parts they find tedious rather than the parts they find
interesting. That distinction mattered a lot to them and they made it three separate times, so
it is worth recording as their actual requirement rather than as a preference.

The tedious parts, as they described them: reconciling the schedule against what the sites
reported afterwards, chasing the sites that reported nothing, and producing the weekly summary
that nobody enjoys assembling. The interesting parts — deciding what the schedule should be in
the first place — they intend to keep, and they were clear that a system which quietly started
making those calls would be turned off rather than argued with.

They also raised a sequencing concern. They do not want to migrate anything until the current
pilot phase closes, because the pilot is being judged on its own numbers and a mid-flight change
would make those numbers unreadable. That is a reasonable position and it sets the earliest
realistic start.

No commitments were made on either side. This is a record of what they asked for and what they
ruled out, so the next conversation does not start from scratch.
