Weekend cover arrangements for the Northwind depots

The weekend cover question that was left open during the Northwind Freight onboarding now has an
answer, and it is worth capturing because the onboarding note explicitly recorded it as
unresolved.

Cover staff move between depots at weekends. Until now each depot briefed its own cover
informally, which worked while the same handful of people covered the same handful of depots and
stopped working the moment the pool grew. The failure was never dramatic: someone arrived, spent
the first hour learning where things were, and the shift ran late. It happened often enough that
people stopped mentioning it.

What the team settled on is that cover staff are briefed against the standard layout rather than
against the depot. A depot that departs from the standard is responsible for saying so in
advance, in writing, rather than expecting the cover to discover it. That puts the cost on the
depot that is different instead of on every person who visits it.

This connects directly to two things already recorded. It closes the open question the onboarding
note left, and it is downstream of the slotting policy — the briefing only works because there is
a standard layout to brief against, and without that decision this arrangement would have nothing
to point at.

Worth revisiting once the remaining depots are on the new arrangement, because the whole thing
assumes the standard is the common case.
