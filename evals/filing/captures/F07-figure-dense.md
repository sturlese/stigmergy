Marlowe Publishing distribution review numbers

Went through the distribution review with Marlowe Publishing. Writing the figures down while they
are still in front of me, because half of them were read off a screen nobody is going to share.

Title throughput. They shipped 214 titles in the review period against a plan of 190, so 24 over.
That over-delivery is not evenly spread: 168 of the 214 came from the trade list and the
remainder from the academic list, where the plan had assumed a much more even split.

Returns. The return rate came in at 11.4 percent overall. On the trade list alone it was 13.2
percent and on the academic list 4.1 percent. They flagged the trade figure as the one they care
about, and noted that the previous period was 12.8 percent, so it moved the wrong way by 0.4
points.

Warehouse. Average time from receipt to shelf was 3.6 days, down from 5.2. They attribute
essentially all of that to the second goods-in bay opening, which came online partway through the
period, so the true steady-state number is probably lower than 3.6 and they will not know until
the next review.

Backlist. 41 percent of revenue came from titles older than two years, which is the highest that
figure has been. Nobody in the room could say whether that is a healthy backlist or a weak
frontlist, and both readings were argued.

One number they specifically asked not to be treated as final: the 214 includes 9 titles that
shipped in the last three days of the period and may yet be pulled back into the next one. So the
throughput figure could reasonably be stated as 205.
