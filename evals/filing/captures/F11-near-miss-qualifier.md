Depot access window changes from Cofers Holdings

Cofers Holdings wrote to say they are changing how their crews book access to our depot
buildings, starting with the next maintenance cycle. Worth recording because the current
arrangement is the one every site has been working to since the standing contract began.

Until now a crew arriving for scheduled work has been let in by whoever was on the gate, and the
visit was written into the site-access register afterwards. Their office wants that reversed: the
visit is booked against a named window before the crew leaves, and a crew arriving outside its
window is turned away rather than let in and logged. Their reason is that they cannot account for
work their own scheduling system never saw, and the register as kept today is the only record
that a visit happened at all.

They were explicit that this is a change to how access is arranged and not to what the standing
contract covers. Nothing about scope, price or response times moves; a site that wants work
outside the contract still agrees that with their office, exactly as before.

The part that affects us is the booking itself. Someone here has to confirm the window, because
their system books against a contact rather than against a building, and the sites do not
currently have a named contact for maintenance visits. Until that is settled the change cannot
start, and their office knows it.

No date was agreed. This is a record of what they asked for and what they said it does not
change, so the next conversation does not start from the beginning.
