Northwind Freight depot rollout status

Caught up with the Northwind team this morning about where the depot rollout actually stands.
The two depots that went first are running on the new routing system and have been for a few
weeks now, with no rollbacks. The dispatch schedules migrated cleanly, which was the piece
everyone expected to be painful, and nothing downstream had to be re-pointed afterwards.

What is left is the remaining depots. There is no date on them yet because the team wants a full
month of the first two running unattended before they commit to a second wave — the reasoning
being that the failures they care about are the ones that only show up over a long weekend, and
they have not had one yet.

Two things came up that are worth writing down. The first is that the routing system's own
reporting is the only place anyone can see whether a depot is actually using it, and nobody owns
that report. The second is that the depots still on the old arrangement have no way of knowing
when their turn comes, so they have stopped asking, which is not the same as being happy.

Nothing here changes any decision already taken. It is a status note so the next person who asks
"where did the Northwind rollout get to" does not have to reconstruct it from a chat thread.
