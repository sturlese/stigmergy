Insurance certificate arrived under a different name to the contract

The renewed insurance certificate for the maintenance work arrived this morning, issued to
"Cofers, S.L.", and the standing contract on file is in a different written form of the same
name. Recording it because the mismatch has now caused a delay twice and nobody has written down
what it actually is.

Their office says the two forms are the same company and always have been — the certificate is
issued in the form the insurer holds, the contract was signed in the form their commercial side
uses, and neither is going to change. They offered to have the insurer reissue it, but were clear
that the next renewal would arrive in the same form again, so a reissue fixes this year and
nothing after it.

What actually stalls is our own check. Whoever files the certificate compares the name on it
against the name on the contract, finds they differ, and stops — which is the correct instinct
and the wrong outcome here. The check needs to be against the company rather than against the
string, and there is currently nothing on file that says which written forms belong to whom.

No process change was agreed. This records what their office confirmed and where the delay
actually comes from, so the next certificate does not stall for the same reason.
