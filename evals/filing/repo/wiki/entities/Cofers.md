# Cofers

## What / Who

Cofers maintains depot buildings and the equipment inside them under a standing contract. The
relationship is operational rather than commercial: most of what gets recorded about them is
scheduling, access and the state of the sites they look after.

## Facts

- Registered under the id `cofers`, and usually written simply as "Cofers" in running prose.
- Their own paperwork does not settle on one written form. Invoices, insurance certificates and
  the site-access register each carry a different legal or group form of the same name, and
  correspondence from their office arrives under whichever one the sender happens to use.
- Works to a standing maintenance contract rather than to individual orders, so a change of
  scope is agreed with their office and not at a site.
- Their crews reach sites through the same access process every visit, which is a recurring
  constraint on anything that has to happen inside a depot.
