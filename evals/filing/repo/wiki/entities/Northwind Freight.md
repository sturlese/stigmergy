# Northwind Freight

## What / Who

Northwind Freight is a regional haulage operator and a customer. It runs depots across several
regions, and most of what is recorded about it concerns depot operations: route planning,
dispatch scheduling and warehouse slotting.

## Facts

- Registered under the id `northwind-freight`, and written as the short form "Northwind" in
  running prose as often as in full.
- Each depot was historically arranged and staffed on its own terms, which is the background to
  most decisions recorded against this entity.
- Its first depots were brought onto the routing system in the work recorded in
  [[Northwind Freight Onboarding]]; the rest are a later phase.
- Depot layouts are governed by [[Warehouse Slotting Policy]].
