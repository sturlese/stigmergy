# Quillon Labs

## What / Who

Quillon Labs is a materials-research partner. The relationship is joint work rather than delivery
against a contract, so most of what is recorded about them concerns how the work itself is run:
their methods, the vocabulary they run it in, and the equipment it depends on.

## Facts

- Registered under the id `quillon-labs`, and usually written as "Quillon" in running prose.
- A research partner rather than a customer: work is agreed collaboratively and their internal
  practice, not ours, governs how it is run.
- Has house usages that do not travel — words that mean something narrower inside Quillon than
  they do elsewhere, and are worth checking rather than assuming.
- Their laboratory equipment is shared and scheduled, which is a recurring constraint on anything
  that depends on their results.
