# Marlowe Publishing

## What / Who

Marlowe Publishing is a trade publisher and a customer. It publishes on two lists — a trade list
and a much smaller academic one — and runs its own warehouse and distribution.

## Facts

- Registered under the id `marlowe-publishing`, and written as "Marlowe" in running prose.
- Publishes on a trade list and an academic list, which behave differently enough that the two
  are usually reported separately.
- Runs its own warehousing and distribution, and reviews both periodically.
- A different line of business from [[Northwind Freight]]'s haulage and [[Quillon Labs]]'s
  research, and shares no work with either.

## Connections

- [[Northwind Freight]] — a customer, in an unrelated line of business
- [[Quillon Labs]] — a research partner, in an unrelated line of business
