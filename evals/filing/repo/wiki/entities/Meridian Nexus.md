# Meridian Nexus

## What / Who

Meridian Nexus supplies the settlement platform our finance team reconciles against. The
relationship is a supplier one, and what gets recorded about it is almost entirely about the
platform's behaviour rather than about the company.

## Facts

- Registered under the id `meridian-nexus`. Almost nobody writes the full name: in running prose,
  in tickets and in meetings it is simply "Nexus", and the longer form appears mainly on
  contracts.
- Supplies the settlement platform only — it runs no depot, holds no site access and has no part
  in maintenance or in legal work.
- Their release notes are the record of what changed in the platform, so a reconciliation that
  starts behaving differently is usually traced there first.
- Support is reached through their own portal rather than by mail, which is why threads about the
  platform rarely carry the company's name at all.
