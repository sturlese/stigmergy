# Cofers Legal

## What / Who

Cofers Legal is an independent practice retained for contract review. It is engaged directly by
our own commercial team and reports to nobody else here.

## Facts

- Registered under the id `cofers-legal`, and always written in full.
- **No relationship whatsoever to [[Cofers]], the maintenance contractor.** The shared word is a
  founder's surname and nothing else: separate ownership, separate offices, separate work, and
  neither one has ever been a party to the other's agreements. Anything filed against one of them
  belongs to that one alone.
- Retained per engagement rather than under a standing arrangement, so each piece of work has its
  own scope letter.
- Correspondence comes from named solicitors rather than from a general office address, which is
  usually the quickest way to tell which of the two names a message is actually from.
