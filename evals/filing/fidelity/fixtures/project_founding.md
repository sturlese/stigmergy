# Northwind warehouse migration kickoff

Kickoff call held on 2026-09-22 between Dana (Initech) and Omar Haddad (Globex Logistics).

Globex Logistics hired Initech to move its three warehouses onto a new warehouse management
system. The migration is called the Northwind warehouse migration. Dana leads it for Initech and
Omar Haddad is the sponsor at Globex.

Agreed today:
- The Lyon site goes first, as a pilot, with a target go-live of 2026-11-15.
- Porto and Gdansk follow after the pilot review; no dates are set for them yet.
- Initech sends a fixed-price proposal for the full migration by 2026-10-01.

Open: Globex has not approved a budget. Omar Haddad said the budget depends on the proposal.
