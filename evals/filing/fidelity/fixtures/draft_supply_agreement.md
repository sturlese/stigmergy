# Halcyon_MSA_Oakline_draft_2026-09-08

MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Made in Rotterdam. This Agreement takes effect on 10 October 2026 (the "Effective Date").

PARTIES

(1) HALCYON ROBOTICS B.V., a company registered in the Netherlands (the "Supplier"), acting through
its managing director, Ms. Ilse de Wit.

(2) OAKLINE LOGISTICS LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (the "Customer"), acting
through its chief operating officer, Mr. Daniel Price.

BACKGROUND

(A) The Supplier delivered two parcel-sorting units to the Customer under a pilot purchase order
dated 3 February 2026. That order is complete and is not changed by this Agreement.

(B) Tessaly Freight has proposed to join this Agreement as a second customer for six units, subject
to approval by its board.

TERMS

1. Supply. The Supplier shall supply forty parcel-sorting units over thirty-six months, in eight
quarterly deliveries starting in the first quarter of 2027.

2. Price. The total price is EUR 2,400,000, invoiced per delivery, with a 5% volume rebate once
twenty units are installed.

3. Term. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs for three years.
