# Series A Term Sheet September 2026

On 18 September 2026 Seabright Partners sent a term sheet for 12 million euros at a pre-money valuation of 50 million euros, investing 9 million itself, with Julian Ashworth joining the board. [[Kittiwake Capital]] takes its pro-rata of 2 million euros and Oxbow Ventures asked for the remaining 1 million. (Source: `sources/2026/09/8b8f0ac8-8040-5084-9fab-ce54d8beba71.md`)

The conditions are the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] production contract and the SOC 2 Type II report. [[Odile Ferrand]] recommended accepting, and closing is planned for mid-October. (Source: `sources/2026/09/8b8f0ac8-8040-5084-9fab-ce54d8beba71.md`)
