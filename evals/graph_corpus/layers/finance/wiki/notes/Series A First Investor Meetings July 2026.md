# Series A First Investor Meetings July 2026

On 24 July 2026 [[Hannah Brandt]] recorded the first Series A meetings. Julian Ashworth of Seabright Partners liked the [[Halberd Mutual]] partnership and wants the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] production contract before a term sheet. Mei Tanaka of Oxbow Ventures asked for gross margin by customer. (Source: `sources/2026/07/4a4670d7-1366-5ae2-b393-a999ba3967dd.md`)

[[Kittiwake Capital]] confirmed its pro-rata commitment of about 2 million euros, and the data room is due by 15 August. (Source: `sources/2026/07/4a4670d7-1366-5ae2-b393-a999ba3967dd.md`)
