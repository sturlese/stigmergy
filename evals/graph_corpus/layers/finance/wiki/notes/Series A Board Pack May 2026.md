# Series A Board Pack May 2026

On 6 May 2026 [[Hannah Brandt]] and [[Ines Okafor]] presented the board with the plan to raise a 12 million euro Series A at a target pre-money valuation of 48 million euros. Annual recurring revenue was 2.6 million euros in April and monthly net burn was 160,000 euros, giving 14 months of runway. (Source: `sources/2026/05/4b30a13b-889b-5b7e-a1bf-26278bdfce7e.md`)

[[Kittiwake Capital]] will invest its pro-rata share of about 2 million euros, and Noor Haddad will introduce potential leads; Seabright Partners and Oxbow Ventures are on the shortlist. Funds go to the Hamburg expansion, the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 hardware, and a pharmaceutical sales team after the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot. [[Odile Ferrand]] advised waiting for the Altamira production contract. (Source: `sources/2026/05/4b30a13b-889b-5b7e-a1bf-26278bdfce7e.md`)
