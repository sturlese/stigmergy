# Amara Nwosu Joins Tidewell as Data Engineer

Announcement from [[Rafael Duarte]], 10 July 2026. (Source: `sources/2026/07/b799fdea-578c-5c1a-8f76-58b921608682.md`)

## Hire

[[Amara Nwosu]] joins [[Tidewell]] as a data engineer, reporting to Rafael Duarte, and works from Lisbon. She previously built grid-monitoring pipelines at [[Voltaic Grid]], an energy analytics company. (Source: `sources/2026/07/b799fdea-578c-5c1a-8f76-58b921608682.md`)

## First Projects

- Take over the monthly excursion summaries sent to [[Halberd Mutual]], which [[Priya Raman]] built by hand.
- Build an analytics store of every temperature reading since 2024, so the team can study how refrigeration units fail.
- Work with Priya Raman on predicting compressor failures from temperature data.
