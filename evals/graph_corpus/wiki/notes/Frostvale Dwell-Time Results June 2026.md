# Frostvale Dwell-Time Results June 2026

On 10 June 2026, [[Tidewell]] reviewed the first full month of dwell-time alerting results with [[Frostvale Distribution]]. Frostvale was represented by [[Gunnar Lindqvist]], VP Logistics; Tidewell by [[Aurélie Fontaine]] and [[Priya Raman]]. (Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

## Rollout

Dwell-time alerting went live for Frostvale on 15 May 2026, as committed in its April renewal. (Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

## Results

For the first full month:

- alerts fell from about 1,900 to 930 a month, a 51 percent reduction, better than the 45 percent predicted in the design;
- the acknowledged-alert ratio rose from 22 percent to 64 percent;
- no excursion that exhausted its budget went unalerted.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

## Follow-Ups

Gunnar said his dispatchers now trust [[Coldpath]] again. He still wants the acknowledged-alert ratio above 70 percent and asked for quiet hours at the two cross-docking hubs in Setúbal and Zaragoza. (Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

## Insurance

[[Halberd Mutual]] confirmed that Frostvale is eligible for the 7 percent premium discount from July, based on the first monthly excursion summaries. (Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)
