# Hannah Brandt Joins Tidewell as CFO

On 18 February 2026, [[Ines Okafor]], CEO of [[Tidewell]], announced internally that [[Hannah Brandt]] joins Tidewell that day as Chief Financial Officer, reporting to Ines. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)

## Background

Hannah was previously finance director at [[Marlow Freight]], a logistics scale-up, where she led the move from per-vehicle to usage-based pricing and ran two funding rounds. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)

## First Six Months

Hannah will own three things in her first six months:

- the financial model for the new pricing per monitored asset, together with [[Mireille Laurent]];
- the budget for the Hamburg office that [[Tomasz Zielinski]] is setting up;
- preparation for the Series A round, working with the existing investor [[Kittiwake Capital]].

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)

She sits with the commercial team in Lisbon on Tuesdays and Thursdays. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)
