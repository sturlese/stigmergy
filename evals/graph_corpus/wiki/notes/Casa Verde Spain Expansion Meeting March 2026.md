# Casa Verde Spain Expansion Meeting March 2026

On 19 March 2026, [[Tidewell]] held an account meeting with [[Casa Verde Markets]], a grocery chain that uses Tidewell on its delivery trucks in Portugal. Casa Verde was represented by [[Beatriz Sousa]], Head of Fresh; Tidewell by [[Mireille Laurent]] and [[Tomasz Zielinski]]. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Expansion and Scope

Casa Verde is opening 65 stores in Andalusia this year. Beatriz wants to monitor the cold rooms inside the stores as well as the trucks: about 1,300 cold rooms across the new and existing stores. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Product Fit

[[Tomasz Zielinski]] confirmed that the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateway supports fixed installation with mains power, which in-store cold rooms would require. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Commercial Terms

Under [[Per-Asset Pricing|per-asset pricing]], the deal would be worth about 260,000 euros of annual recurring revenue, which would make Casa Verde Tidewell's second-largest customer. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Competition

Beatriz raised a concern: [[Brightline Telematics]] has offered Casa Verde a bundle of GPS tracking and temperature monitoring at a lower price per truck. She asked Tidewell to explain why it is worth more. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Next Steps

- [[Mireille Laurent]] will send a proposal by 2 April.
- [[Tomasz Zielinski]] will survey five stores in Seville to plan the installation.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)
