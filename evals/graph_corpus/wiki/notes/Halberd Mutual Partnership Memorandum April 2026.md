# Halberd Mutual Partnership Memorandum April 2026

On 2 April 2026, [[Tidewell]] and [[Halberd Mutual]] signed a memorandum of understanding. Halberd Mutual was represented by [[Oliver Pennington]], Head of Partnerships; Tidewell by [[Ines Okafor]] and [[Hannah Brandt]]. (Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

## Terms

Halberd Mutual will give a 7 percent discount on cargo insurance premiums to fleets that use Tidewell with [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]] alerting switched on. (Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

## Data Sharing

Data sharing is deliberately narrow. Each month Tidewell sends Halberd an excursion summary per insured fleet: the number of excursions, minutes out of range, and whether the budget was exhausted. No raw readings and no vehicle locations are shared. Each customer must opt in. (Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

## First Customer

The first joint customer will be [[Frostvale Distribution]], which already insures its cargo with Halberd. [[Oliver Pennington]] estimated Frostvale's saving at about 48,000 euros a year. (Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

## Competitive Rationale

[[Hannah Brandt]] sees the partnership as the answer to [[Brightline Telematics]] in price-driven deals: the insurance saving is larger than the price gap per truck. (Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)
