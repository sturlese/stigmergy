# Halden Seafood Win-Back Call June 2026

On 17 June 2026, [[Sigrid Aas]], operations manager at [[Halden Seafood]], called [[Mireille Laurent]] of [[Tidewell]] about returning to Tidewell. (Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

## Background

Halden Seafood left Tidewell for [[Brightline Telematics]] in January 2026 because of price. (Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

## Spoiled Shipments

In May 2026, two salmon shipments spoiled without any alert from Brightline: the temperature rose slowly over several hours but never crossed Brightline's threshold for long enough to trigger it. Halden lost about 40,000 euros of product. (Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

## Excursion Budget Question

Sigrid had read about [[Excursion Budget|excursion budgets]] and asked whether Tidewell would have caught those shipments. Mireille explained that a slow rise consumes the excursion budget and would have raised an alert at 50 percent. (Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

## Insurance

Sigrid also asked about the [[Halberd Mutual]] discount, since Halden is shopping for a new cargo insurer after the claims. (Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

## Proposal

Halden would return with 60 trucks on [[Per-Asset Pricing|per-asset pricing]], starting with a one-month pilot in July 2026. Mireille will send terms by 24 June. (Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)
