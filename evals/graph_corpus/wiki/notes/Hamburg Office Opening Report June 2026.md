# Hamburg Office Opening Report June 2026

Report from [[Sabine Krüger]], sales lead for Germany, Austria and Switzerland, on 3 June 2026. (Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

## Office Opening

[[Tidewell]]'s Hamburg office opened on 1 June 2026 with Sabine and [[Lukas Becker]], field engineer. [[Tomasz Zielinski]] spent the first week with them. (Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

## Pipeline

After the first week, 9 German frozen-food distributors are in conversation. [[Odile Ferrand]] introduced two of them personally. The most advanced is [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl]], a distributor with 150 trucks in northern Germany, which wants a pilot in September. (Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

## What German Prospects Ask

- Where the data is stored — answered by the Frankfurt region of [[Cumulus Cloud]].
- Whether Tidewell has a SOC 2 report — expected in late September.
- References — Sabine uses the [[Frostvale Distribution]] results on alert noise, with [[Gunnar Lindqvist]]'s permission.
