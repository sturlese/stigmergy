# Stratos Software Partnership Call July 2026

Partnership call on 14 July 2026 between [[Tidewell]] and [[Stratos Software]], maker of [[Stratos ERP]], an enterprise system used by food distributors in Germany and the Netherlands. Stratos was represented by [[Pieter Janssen]], partner manager; Tidewell by [[Sabine Krüger]] and [[Rafael Duarte]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

## Agreement

Tidewell will build a certified Stratos connector on top of the [[Frostline API]]. Excursion events and Good Distribution Practice reports will attach to the matching shipment record in [[Stratos ERP]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

## Marketplace and Customers

[[Pieter Janssen]] said about 60 Stratos customers run refrigerated fleets, and Stratos will list Tidewell in its partner marketplace once the connector passes certification. The first joint customer is [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl]]. [[Casa Verde Markets]], which also runs Stratos ERP in its Spanish warehouses, will follow. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

## Effort

[[Rafael Duarte]] estimates six weeks of work for the connector. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)
