# Tidewell 2026 Kickoff

Tidewell's leadership held its 2026 kickoff on 8 January 2026 in the Lisbon office. Present were Ines Okafor (CEO and co-founder), Rafael Duarte (CTO and co-founder), Mireille Laurent (Head of Sales) and Tomasz Zielinski (Head of Operations). (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## Business Position

Tidewell sells cold-chain monitoring for refrigerated transport: the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateway sits in each truck, reads temperature probes and door sensors, and streams readings to [[Coldpath]], the web dashboard dispatchers use. Customers can also pull data through the [[Frostline API]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

Annual recurring revenue closed 2025 at 2.1 million euros across 31 customers. [[Frostvale Distribution]] alone is 38 percent of ARR, which the team agreed is a concentration risk. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## 2026 Priorities

The four priorities agreed for 2026:

1. Sign a pilot with [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] to enter pharmaceutical distribution; [[Mireille Laurent]] owns it.
2. Open a Hamburg office by June to sell to German frozen-food distributors; [[Tomasz Zielinski]] owns the setup.
3. Pass a SOC 2 Type II audit by September, because pharma customers require it; [[Rafael Duarte]] owns it with the security lead, [[Dmitri Volkov]].
4. Replace the flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month with pricing per monitored asset; [[Mireille Laurent]] and the incoming CFO own the design.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

[[Rafael Duarte]] added that [[Joon-ho Park]] will redesign the [[Coldpath]] dashboard in the first half, because dispatchers at [[Frostvale Distribution]] complain that they cannot find the alerts that matter. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)
