# Altamira Pharma Logistics Pilot Kickoff

On 4 March 2026, [[Tidewell]] and [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] held the kickoff for their pilot. Altamira was represented by [[Kwame Mensah]], Quality Director; Tidewell by [[Mireille Laurent]], [[Tomasz Zielinski]] and [[Priya Raman]]. (Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

## Scope

The pilot covers 40 delivery vans based in Porto and Madrid for 90 days. Altamira distributes vaccines and biologics in Portugal and Spain, and the products must stay between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. (Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

## Success Criteria

Dr. Mensah set three success criteria:

- zero excursions that Tidewell fails to detect;
- reports that Altamira's quality team can file for Good Distribution Practice audits without rework;
- alerts based on an excursion budget of 60 cumulative minutes per shipment, matching Altamira's own procedures.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

## Probe Sourcing

Dr. Mensah required that no probe from [[Veldt Sensors]] batch VS-2207 be used in the pilot, after hearing about the drift problem. [[Tomasz Zielinski]] confirmed that all 80 pilot probes come from a newer batch with individual calibration certificates. (Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

## Commercial Terms

The pilot fee is 18,000 euros. If the pilot succeeds, Altamira will discuss a production contract, but Dr. Mensah said their procurement will require a SOC 2 Type II report first. (Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)
