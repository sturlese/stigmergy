# Casa Verde Cold-Room Rollout Phase 1 July 2026

On 21 July 2026, [[Tidewell]] and [[Casa Verde Markets]] held a status meeting on phase 1 of the cold-room rollout. Casa Verde was represented by [[Beatriz Sousa]], Head of Fresh; Tidewell by [[Tomasz Zielinski]], Head of Operations. The installer is [[Nuno Carvalho]] of [[Refritec Instalações]], the refrigeration contractor Casa Verde uses in its stores. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)

## Scope and Progress

Phase 1 covers 400 cold rooms in 30 stores in Andalusia. [[Refritec Instalações]] installs the probes and mounts the gateways; [[Tidewell]] configures the sites in [[Coldpath]]. As of the meeting, 180 cold rooms are live in 14 stores. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)

## Connectivity Problem

In 9 stores the cold rooms sit behind thick walls where Wi-Fi does not reach, and the current [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v2 gateway has no cellular fallback for fixed installations. [[Tomasz Zielinski]] proposed using the Sentinel v3 pilot units in those stores, since v3 has LTE-M. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)

## Next Steps

[[Beatriz Sousa]] is satisfied with the pace and asked that phase 2, another 900 cold rooms, start in October. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)
