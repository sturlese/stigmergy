# Frostvale Renewal Negotiation April 2026

On 9 April 2026, [[Tidewell]] and [[Frostvale Distribution]] held a renewal meeting. Frostvale was represented by [[Gunnar Lindqvist]], VP Logistics; Tidewell by [[Mireille Laurent]], [[Hannah Brandt]] and [[Aurélie Fontaine]]. (Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

## Outcome

Frostvale renews for two years. (Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

## Terms

- Frostvale moves to [[Per-Asset Pricing]]. Its bill rises 8 percent, below the 10 percent first-year cap agreed at the [[Pricing Workshop February 2026]].
- Tidewell commits to ship [[Dwell-Time Awareness|dwell-time alerting]] by 15 May 2026, which Gunnar had asked for in January.
- Frostvale opts in to the [[Halberd Mutual]] discount programme.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

## Probe Replacement

Gunnar confirmed that [[Veldt Sensors]] finished replacing the 180 drifting probes from batch VS-2207 in Frostvale trucks on 28 March, two days early. (Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

## Coldpath Redesign

Gunnar said the [[Coldpath]] redesign demo convinced his dispatchers more than any slide. [[Aurélie Fontaine]] will run training sessions for Frostvale's three dispatch centres after the redesign ships on 20 April. (Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)
