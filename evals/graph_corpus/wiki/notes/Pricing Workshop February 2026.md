# Pricing Workshop February 2026

On 26 February 2026, [[Tidewell]] held a pricing workshop with [[Mireille Laurent]], [[Hannah Brandt]] and [[Ines Okafor]]. The workshop agreed to replace the flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month with pricing per monitored asset. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## Current Pricing

Today every customer pays a flat 39 euros per truck per month, whatever the truck carries. A truck with one probe pays the same as a truck with six probes and two door sensors, and fixed cold rooms in stores cannot be priced at all. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## Proposed Pricing

Under the agreed proposal, each probe or door sensor costs 14 euros per month and each [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateway 9 euros per month. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## Modelled Effect

[[Hannah Brandt]] modelled the effect on current customers:

- [[Frostvale Distribution]], whose trucks carry several probes each, would pay about 12 percent more.
- [[Casa Verde Markets]], with simpler trucks, would pay about 5 percent less.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## Decisions

- New customers get per-asset pricing from 1 April 2026.
- Existing customers move to it at their next renewal, with the increase capped at 10 percent in the first year.
- [[Mireille Laurent]] will pitch it as land-and-expand: a customer can start by monitoring a few trucks and grow into cold rooms and more sensors without renegotiating the contract.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)
