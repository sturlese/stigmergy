# First-Half 2026 Review June 2026

On 30 June 2026, [[Ines Okafor]], CEO of [[Tidewell]], sent a memo to all staff reviewing the first half of 2026 and setting priorities for the second half. (Source: `sources/2026/06/f0965a0c-1c57-529c-a4f0-679d12f235f1.md`)

## Business Position

Annual recurring revenue reached 2.9 million euros at the end of June, up from 2.1 million in January. (Source: `sources/2026/06/f0965a0c-1c57-529c-a4f0-679d12f235f1.md`)

## Delivered in the First Half

- [[Frostvale Distribution]] renewed for two years, and dwell-time alerting halved its alert volume.
- [[Casa Verde Markets]] signed the Spanish expansion, including cold rooms in 65 new stores.
- The [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot succeeded; the production contract waits for Tidewell's SOC 2 report.
- The Hamburg office opened on 1 June.
- [[Per-Asset Pricing]] is live for all new customers.
- The [[Halberd Mutual]] partnership gives Tidewell customers a 7 percent insurance discount.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/f0965a0c-1c57-529c-a4f0-679d12f235f1.md`)

## Second-Half Priorities

1. Deliver the SOC 2 Type II report in September and sign [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]].
2. Ship [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 in October.
3. Close the first German customers.
4. Win back [[Halden Seafood]].
5. Raise the Series A.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/f0965a0c-1c57-529c-a4f0-679d12f235f1.md`)
