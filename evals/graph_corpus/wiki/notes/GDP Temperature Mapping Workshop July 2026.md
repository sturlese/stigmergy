# GDP Temperature Mapping Workshop July 2026

On 17 July 2026, [[Tidewell]] and [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] held a workshop on temperature mapping. Altamira was represented by [[Kwame Mensah]], Quality Director, and [[Elena Marín]], compliance officer; Tidewell by [[Priya Raman]] and [[Joon-ho Park]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

## Good Distribution Practice Requirement

[[Elena Marín]] explained that Good Distribution Practice expects temperature mapping of every vehicle type: a study that places several probes across the cargo space to find hot and cold spots, repeated each summer and winter. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

## Current Practice

Today Altamira runs mapping studies with a consultant and paper reports, which takes about two weeks per van type. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

## Proposal

Tidewell proposes to generate mapping reports automatically from vans fitted with extra probes during a mapping week, showing the temperature distribution by position and the warmest location. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

## Follow-Up

[[Elena Marín]] will send the report format that Altamira's inspectors accept. [[Joon-ho Park]] will design the report; [[Priya Raman]] estimates it can be ready by the end of August. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)
