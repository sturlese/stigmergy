# Customer Advisory Board First Session July 2026

On 28 July 2026, [[Tidewell]] held the first session of its customer advisory board, hosted by [[Ines Okafor]]. The members were [[Gunnar Lindqvist]] of [[Frostvale Distribution]], [[Beatriz Sousa]] of [[Casa Verde Markets]], [[Kwame Mensah]] of [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] and [[Klaus Ehrlich]] of [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)

## Roadmap Ranking

Each member ranked the roadmap:

1. Predicting refrigeration failures before they happen. [[Gunnar Lindqvist]] said a failed compressor costs [[Frostvale Distribution]] about 25,000 euros of product per incident.
2. Multi-temperature compartments, for trucks that carry frozen and chilled goods at once. [[Beatriz Sousa]] needs this for [[Casa Verde Markets]]' mixed deliveries.
3. Automatic temperature mapping reports, which [[Kwame Mensah]] called essential for pharma.
4. A mobile app for drivers.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)

## Board Governance

[[Klaus Ehrlich]] asked that the board meet twice a year and that members see the roadmap before other customers. (Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
