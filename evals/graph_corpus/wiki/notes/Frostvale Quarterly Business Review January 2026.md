# Frostvale Quarterly Business Review January 2026

[[Tidewell]] and [[Frostvale Distribution]] held a quarterly business review on 15 January 2026. Frostvale was represented by [[Gunnar Lindqvist]], VP Logistics; Tidewell by [[Aurélie Fontaine]], customer success manager for the account, and [[Mireille Laurent]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

## Fleet and Contract

Frostvale runs 420 refrigerated trucks, all fitted with [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateways, delivering frozen food to supermarkets in Portugal and Spain. Its contract with Tidewell renews in April 2026. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

## Alert Noise

Gunnar's main complaint was alert noise. In December his dispatchers received 1,900 temperature alerts; 70 percent resolved on their own within ten minutes, usually because a door was opened during a planned delivery. Dispatchers acknowledged only 22 percent of alerts, and Gunnar said his team has started ignoring [[Coldpath]] notifications altogether. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

Gunnar asked for two changes: alerts that understand planned stops at loading docks, which he called [[Dwell-Time Awareness|dwell-time awareness]], and a single view per trip instead of a flat list of alerts. He said Frostvale will not renew on the current terms unless the alert noise drops. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

## Insurance

Gunnar said Frostvale's cargo insurer, [[Halberd Mutual]], has started offering premium discounts to fleets that can prove continuous temperature monitoring, and asked whether Tidewell reports could serve as that proof. Aurélie will follow up. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)
