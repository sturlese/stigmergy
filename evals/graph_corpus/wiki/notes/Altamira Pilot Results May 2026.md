# Altamira Pilot Results May 2026

On 20 May 2026, [[Tidewell]] and [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] reviewed the results of their 90-day pilot. Altamira was represented by [[Kwame Mensah]], Quality Director; Tidewell by [[Mireille Laurent]] and [[Priya Raman]]. (Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

## Results

The pilot covered 40 delivery vans based in Porto and Madrid. It monitored 11,800 trips and detected 23 temperature excursions. 21 of the 23 stayed within the 60-minute [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]]; the 2 that exhausted it were both caused by a van door left open at the Madrid depot. Altamira checked the results against its own data loggers and found zero excursions Tidewell failed to detect. (Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

Altamira's quality team filed Tidewell's Good Distribution Practice reports in an internal audit without rework, which Dr. Mensah called the most important result. (Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

## Decision

Altamira will move to a production contract covering 310 vans across Portugal and Spain from September 2026, worth about 190,000 euros of annual recurring revenue. The contract is conditional on Tidewell delivering its SOC 2 Type II report. (Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

## Follow-Up

[[Priya Raman]] will write up the Madrid door incidents with [[Dmitri Volkov]] as a blameless postmortem. (Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)
