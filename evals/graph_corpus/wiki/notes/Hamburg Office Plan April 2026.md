# Hamburg Office Plan April 2026

Planning meeting on 16 April 2026 with [[Ines Okafor]], [[Tomasz Zielinski]] and [[Hannah Brandt]]. (Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

## Office and Market

[[Tidewell]] will open its Hamburg office in HafenCity on 1 June 2026. The goal is to sell to German frozen-food distributors, a market roughly three times the size of Portugal and Spain combined. (Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

## First Hires

- [[Sabine Krüger]], sales lead for Germany, Austria and Switzerland, joining from a refrigerated logistics company.
- [[Lukas Becker]], field engineer, who will install gateways and run calibration audits in Germany.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

## Budget

The office's 2026 budget is 420,000 euros, approved by [[Hannah Brandt]]. (Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

## Data Residency

German customers expect their data to stay in Germany. [[Rafael Duarte]] confirmed that [[Cumulus Cloud]] has a Frankfurt region, and the second region planned after the February outage will be Frankfurt, which covers both failover and data residency. (Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

## Introductions

[[Odile Ferrand]], an angel investor and board observer who spent twenty years in German cold storage, offered to introduce Sabine to distributors. (Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)
