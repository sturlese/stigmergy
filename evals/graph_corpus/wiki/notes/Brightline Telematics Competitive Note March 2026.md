# Brightline Telematics Competitive Note March 2026

[[Mireille Laurent]], Head of Sales at [[Tidewell]], wrote a competitive note for the sales team on 27 March 2026 about [[Brightline Telematics]]. (Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

## Brightline's Offer

Brightline sells GPS tracking for trucks and bundles temperature monitoring into it. Its list price is 29 euros per truck per month, which undercuts Tidewell's old flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month. (Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

## Weaknesses

- It alerts on every threshold crossing and has no [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]], so customers get the same alert noise that [[Frostvale Distribution]] complained about.
- It does not produce Good Distribution Practice reports, so it cannot serve pharmaceutical distributors such as [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]].
- Its temperature hardware is resold and has no per-probe calibration certificates.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

## Customer Loss

Brightline won one Tidewell customer: [[Halden Seafood]] switched to Brightline in January 2026. [[Sigrid Aas]], Halden's operations manager, said the decision was about price alone. (Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

## Response

Mireille recommends leading with compliance and with the cost of spoiled cargo, not with price per truck. She also recommends a partnership with an insurer, because an insurance discount can outweigh the price difference. [[Hannah Brandt]] is exploring this with [[Halberd Mutual]]. (Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)
