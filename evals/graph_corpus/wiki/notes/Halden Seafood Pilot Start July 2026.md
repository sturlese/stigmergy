# Halden Seafood Pilot Start July 2026

On 7 July 2026, [[Halden Seafood]] started its one-month pilot with [[Tidewell]], following the win-back call in June. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)

## Participants

Halden was represented by [[Sigrid Aas]], operations manager, and [[Tiago Ferreira]], quality assurance manager; Tidewell by [[Mireille Laurent]] and [[Aurélie Fontaine]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)

## Scope

The pilot covers 60 trucks carrying fresh salmon from the port of Aveiro to supermarkets in Portugal. [[Excursion Budget|Excursion budgets]] are set at 20 cumulative minutes above 4 degrees Celsius, the limit Tiago's quality team uses for fresh fish. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)

## Insurance Onboarding

Halden also started onboarding with [[Halberd Mutual]] as its new cargo insurer. [[Oliver Pennington]] joined the call and confirmed that Halden becomes eligible for the 7 percent premium discount after two months of excursion summaries. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)

## Training

[[Aurélie Fontaine]] trained Halden's four dispatchers on the [[Coldpath]] trip view. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)
