# Temperature Mapping

## Definition

Temperature mapping is a study that places several probes across a vehicle's cargo space to find hot and cold spots, required by Good Distribution Practice for every vehicle type and repeated each summer and winter. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

## How It Works

A mapping study places several probes across the cargo space to find the hot and cold spots of a vehicle type, and is repeated each summer and winter. [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] currently runs mapping studies with a consultant and paper reports, which takes about two weeks per van type. [[Tidewell]] proposes to generate mapping reports automatically from vans fitted with extra probes during a mapping week, showing the temperature distribution by position and the warmest location. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

## Why It Matters

Good Distribution Practice expects temperature mapping of every vehicle type, and inspectors accept a specific report format that [[Elena Marín]] will send. The source does not report the number of probes used, the acceptance criteria, or the cost of the current consultant studies. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)
