# Per-Asset Pricing

## Definition

Per-asset pricing is a pricing model in which a customer pays a monthly fee for each monitored asset rather than a flat fee per vehicle. At [[Tidewell]] the monitored assets are temperature probes, door sensors and [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateways. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## How It Works

Each probe or door sensor costs 14 euros per month and each Sentinel gateway 9 euros per month. It replaces the flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month, under which a truck with one probe paid the same as a truck with six probes and two door sensors, and fixed cold rooms in stores could not be priced at all. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## Why It Matters

Per-asset pricing lets a customer start small and add monitored assets without renegotiating the contract, which [[Mireille Laurent]] will pitch as land-and-expand. New customers get it from 1 April 2026; existing customers move at their next renewal, with the increase capped at 10 percent in the first year. The source does not report the total revenue effect or how many customers would pay more or less. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

## Examples

- [[Frostvale Distribution]]: its trucks carry several probes each, so it would pay about 12 percent more.
- [[Casa Verde Markets]]: its trucks are simpler, so it would pay about 5 percent less.
- [[Casa Verde Markets]]: a deal covering about 1,300 in-store cold rooms would be worth about 260,000 euros of annual recurring revenue, which would make it [[Tidewell]]'s second-largest customer.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

- 2026-04-09: [[Frostvale Distribution]] moved to it at renewal; its bill rises 8 percent, below the 10 percent first-year cap.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)
