# Calibration Audit

## Definition

A calibration audit is a periodic check in which a sample of temperature probes is compared against reference thermometers to detect measurement drift. (Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

## How It Works

[[Tidewell]] decided on 10 February 2026 to run a calibration audit every quarter, comparing a sample of probes against reference thermometers. The source does not report the sample size, the acceptance threshold, or who owns the audit. (Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

## Why It Matters

The audit is a response to probe drift in batch VS-2207, which field engineers found only by comparing probes against reference thermometers at [[Frostvale Distribution]]. A recurring sample check is meant to catch drift before it affects a whole fleet. (Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)
