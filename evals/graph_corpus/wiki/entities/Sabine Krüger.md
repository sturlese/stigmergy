# Sabine Krüger

## What / Who

Sabine Krüger is the sales lead for Germany, Austria and Switzerland at [[Tidewell]], joining from a refrigerated logistics company. (Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-04-16: named as a first hire for Tidewell's Hamburg office, as sales lead for Germany, Austria and Switzerland, joining from a refrigerated logistics company.
- 2026-04-16: [[Odile Ferrand]] offered to introduce her to German frozen-food distributors.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

- 2026-06-01: opened the Hamburg office with [[Lukas Becker]], field engineer; [[Tomasz Zielinski]] spent the first week with them.
- 2026-06-03: reported 9 German frozen-food distributors in conversation after the first week, two of them introduced personally by [[Odile Ferrand]]; the most advanced is [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl]], which wants a pilot in September.
- 2026-06-03: uses the [[Frostvale Distribution]] results on alert noise as references, with [[Gunnar Lindqvist]]'s permission.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

- 2026-07-02: at the [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl Pilot Kickoff]], confirmed that the [[Frostline API]] can push excursion events to [[Stratos ERP]] and that Tidewell is talking to [[Stratos Software]] about a certified connector.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/c54967da-3d89-5c8d-b202-c1056f8f92e4.md`)

- 2026-07-14: represented [[Tidewell]] on a partnership call with [[Stratos Software]] about a certified connector between the [[Frostline API]] and [[Stratos ERP]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)
