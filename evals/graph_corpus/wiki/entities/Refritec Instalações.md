# Refritec Instalações

## What / Who

Refritec Instalações is a refrigeration contractor that [[Casa Verde Markets]] uses in its stores. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-07-21: installs the probes and mounts the gateways for phase 1 of the [[Casa Verde Markets]] cold-room rollout, covering 400 cold rooms in 30 stores in Andalusia, while [[Tidewell]] configures the sites in [[Coldpath]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)
