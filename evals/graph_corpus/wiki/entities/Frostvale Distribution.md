# Frostvale Distribution

## What / Who

Frostvale Distribution is a customer of [[Tidewell]] and its largest by revenue. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## Facts

- 2025: accounted for 38 percent of Tidewell's annual recurring revenue, which the Tidewell leadership team agreed is a concentration risk.
- 2026-01-08: its dispatchers complained that they cannot find the alerts that matter in the [[Coldpath]] dashboard.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

- 2026-01-15: runs 420 refrigerated trucks, all fitted with [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateways, delivering frozen food to supermarkets in Portugal and Spain.
- 2026-01-15: its contract with Tidewell renews in April 2026; [[Gunnar Lindqvist]] said it will not renew on current terms unless alert noise drops.
- 2026-01-15: in December its dispatchers received 1,900 temperature alerts, 70 percent of which resolved on their own within ten minutes, and acknowledged only 22 percent of them.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

- 2026-01-22: an internal memo cited its 1,900 December alerts as the motivation for proposing excursion budgets.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/3e586328-f506-5919-af51-2773aa286315.md`)

- 2026-02-01: was among 14 Tidewell customers affected by the [[Cumulus Cloud]] eu-west outage, during which no alerts were sent; no readings were lost because its [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateways buffered them locally.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/059b5629-3b54-5ba3-821e-08fcb2c7146f.md`)

- 2026-02-10: 180 of the roughly 600 probes from batch VS-2207 are installed in its trucks; [[Veldt Sensors]] will finish replacing them by 30 March.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

- 2026-02-26: modelled to pay about 12 percent more under per-asset pricing, because its trucks carry several probes each.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

- 2026-03-12: three of its dispatchers took part in a usability test of the redesigned [[Coldpath]] home screen.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/6b6348b1-877b-5da1-bc95-849a8a3c6ede.md`)

- 2026-04-02: named the first joint customer under the [[Halberd Mutual]] partnership; [[Oliver Pennington]] estimated it would save about 48,000 euros a year on cargo insurance.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

- 2026-03-28: [[Veldt Sensors]] finished replacing the 180 drifting probes from batch VS-2207 in its trucks, two days early.
- 2026-04-09: renewed its contract with [[Tidewell]] for two years.
- 2026-04-09: moved to [[Per-Asset Pricing]]; its bill rises 8 percent, below the 10 percent first-year cap agreed at the [[Pricing Workshop February 2026]].
- 2026-04-09: opted in to the [[Halberd Mutual]] discount programme.
- 2026-04-09: [[Gunnar Lindqvist]] said the [[Coldpath]] redesign demo convinced his dispatchers more than any slide; [[Aurélie Fontaine]] will run training sessions for its three dispatch centres after the redesign ships on 20 April.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

- 2026-04-23: a design document by [[Priya Raman]] expects dwell-time alerting to cut its alert volume by about 45 percent without missing any excursion that consumes the budget; rollout starts with it on 15 May 2026.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/ce9fdf9b-593d-5421-ac1a-3685218be795.md`)

- 2026-06-03: its results on alert noise are used as references by [[Sabine Krüger]] for German prospects, with [[Gunnar Lindqvist]]'s permission.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

- 2026-06-10: in the first full month after dwell-time alerting went live on 15 May 2026, its alerts fell from about 1,900 to 930 a month, a 51 percent reduction, better than the 45 percent predicted in the design; its acknowledged-alert ratio rose from 22 percent to 64 percent; and no excursion that exhausted its budget went unalerted.
- 2026-06-10: [[Gunnar Lindqvist]] said its dispatchers now trust [[Coldpath]] again; he still wants the acknowledged-alert ratio above 70 percent and asked for quiet hours at the two cross-docking hubs in Setúbal and Zaragoza.
- 2026-06-10: [[Halberd Mutual]] confirmed it is eligible for the 7 percent premium discount from July, based on the first monthly excursion summaries.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

- 2026-07-28: represented at the first session of Tidewell's customer advisory board by [[Gunnar Lindqvist]], who ranked predicting refrigeration failures before they happen first; a failed compressor costs Frostvale about 25,000 euros of product per incident.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
