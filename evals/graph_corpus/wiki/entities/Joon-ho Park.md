# Joon-ho Park

## What / Who

Joon-ho Park is responsible for redesigning the [[Coldpath]] dashboard at [[Tidewell]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-08: reported as set to redesign the [[Coldpath]] dashboard in the first half of 2026, in response to dispatcher complaints at [[Frostvale Distribution]] that they cannot find the alerts that matter.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

- 2026-03-12: presented the redesigned Coldpath home screen at a design review: an [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]] gauge per active shipment, an alert inbox grouped by trip, and quiet hours per site.
- 2026-03-12: ran a usability test with six dispatchers from [[Frostvale Distribution]] and [[Casa Verde Markets]]; the time to find the one shipment that needed action fell from 4 minutes 10 seconds to 1 minute 50 seconds, and five of six preferred the trip grouping.
- 2026-03-12: added a weekly email summary for customers who do not open Coldpath daily to the backlog, at [[Aurélie Fontaine]]'s request.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/6b6348b1-877b-5da1-bc95-849a8a3c6ede.md`)

- 2026-04-30: co-wrote a [[Tidewell]] blog article on [[Alert Fatigue|alert fatigue]] in cold-chain operations with [[Priya Raman]].

(Source: `sources/2026/04/76cb1b34-48f6-50d9-9cc0-199d77e64b7a.md`)

- 2026-07-17: at a temperature-mapping workshop with [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]], took on designing the automatic mapping report.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)
