# Kwame Mensah

## What / Who

Kwame Mensah is Quality Director at [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]], which distributes vaccines and biologics in Portugal and Spain. (Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-03-04: at the pilot kickoff with [[Tidewell]], set three success criteria: zero excursions Tidewell fails to detect, reports Altamira's quality team can file for Good Distribution Practice audits without rework, and alerts based on an excursion budget of 60 cumulative minutes per shipment.
- 2026-03-04: required that no probe from [[Veldt Sensors]] batch VS-2207 be used in the pilot, after hearing about the drift problem.
- 2026-03-04: said Altamira's procurement will require a SOC 2 Type II report before discussing a production contract.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

- 2026-05-20: at the pilot results review, called Altamira's quality team filing Tidewell's Good Distribution Practice reports without rework the most important result.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

- 2026-07-17: attended a temperature-mapping workshop with [[Tidewell]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

- 2026-07-28: at the first session of Tidewell's customer advisory board, ranked automatic temperature mapping reports third and called them essential for pharma.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
