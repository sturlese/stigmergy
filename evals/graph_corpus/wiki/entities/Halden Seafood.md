# Halden Seafood

## What / Who

Halden Seafood is a customer that [[Tidewell]] lost to [[Brightline Telematics]]. Its operations manager is [[Sigrid Aas]]. (Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

Its quality assurance manager is [[Tiago Ferreira]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01: switched from [[Tidewell]] to [[Brightline Telematics]]; [[Sigrid Aas]] said the decision was about price alone.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

- 2026-05: two salmon shipments spoiled without any alert from [[Brightline Telematics]]; the temperature rose slowly over several hours but never crossed Brightline's threshold for long enough to trigger it, and Halden lost about 40,000 euros of product.
- 2026-06-17: [[Sigrid Aas]] called [[Mireille Laurent]] about returning to [[Tidewell]]; Halden proposed returning with 60 trucks on [[Per-Asset Pricing|per-asset pricing]], starting with a one-month pilot in July 2026.
- 2026-06-17: is shopping for a new cargo insurer after the claims.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

- 2026-06-30: named a second-half priority to win back, in the first-half review.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/f0965a0c-1c57-529c-a4f0-679d12f235f1.md`)

- 2026-07-07: started its one-month pilot with [[Tidewell]], covering 60 trucks carrying fresh salmon from the port of Aveiro to supermarkets in Portugal, with [[Excursion Budget|excursion budgets]] set at 20 cumulative minutes above 4 degrees Celsius.
- 2026-07-07: started onboarding with [[Halberd Mutual]] as its new cargo insurer; [[Oliver Pennington]] confirmed it becomes eligible for the 7 percent premium discount after two months of excursion summaries.
- 2026-07-07: [[Aurélie Fontaine]] trained its four dispatchers on the [[Coldpath]] trip view.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)
