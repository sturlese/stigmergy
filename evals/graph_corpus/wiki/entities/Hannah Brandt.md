# Hannah Brandt

## What / Who

Hannah Brandt is Chief Financial Officer at [[Tidewell]], reporting to CEO [[Ines Okafor]]. She was previously finance director at [[Marlow Freight]], a logistics scale-up, where she led the move from per-vehicle to usage-based pricing and ran two funding rounds. (Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-02-18: joined Tidewell as Chief Financial Officer, reporting to [[Ines Okafor]].
- 2026-02-18: in her first six months she will own the financial model for pricing per monitored asset with [[Mireille Laurent]], the budget for the Hamburg office [[Tomasz Zielinski]] is setting up, and preparation for the Series A round with existing investor [[Kittiwake Capital]].
- 2026-02-18: sits with the commercial team in Lisbon on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)

- 2026-02-26: modelled the effect of per-asset pricing on current customers: [[Frostvale Distribution]] would pay about 12 percent more and [[Casa Verde Markets]] about 5 percent less.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

- 2026-03-27: is exploring a partnership with [[Halberd Mutual]], following [[Mireille Laurent]]'s recommendation that an insurance discount can outweigh [[Brightline Telematics]]' price difference.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

- 2026-04-02: signed the memorandum of understanding with [[Halberd Mutual]]; she sees the partnership as the answer to [[Brightline Telematics]] in price-driven deals, because the insurance saving is larger than the price gap per truck.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

- 2026-04-09: represented Tidewell at the [[Frostvale Distribution]] renewal meeting.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

- 2026-04-16: approved the Hamburg office's 2026 budget of 420,000 euros.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

- 2026-08-04: took part in the SOC 2 tabletop incident exercise.

(Source: `sources/2026/08/64c1d91a-92fc-55a1-8f95-cf07cb0e9a72.md`)
