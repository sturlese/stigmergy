# Elena Marín

## What / Who

Elena Marín is compliance officer at [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]], which distributes vaccines and biologics in Portugal and Spain. (Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)
