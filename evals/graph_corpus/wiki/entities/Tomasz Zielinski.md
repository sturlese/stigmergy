# Tomasz Zielinski

## What / Who

Tomasz Zielinski is Head of Operations at [[Tidewell]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-08: took ownership of opening a Hamburg office by June 2026 to sell to German frozen-food distributors.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

- 2026-02-10: reported to [[Veldt Sensors]] that probes from production batch VS-2207 read about 1.4 degrees Celsius too warm after roughly nine months in service, found by comparing probes against reference thermometers at [[Frostvale Distribution]].
- 2026-02-10: will keep a register of probe batches per customer so that a future defect can be traced in hours instead of weeks.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

- 2026-02-18: the budget for the Hamburg office he is setting up will be owned by [[Hannah Brandt]] in her first six months as Chief Financial Officer.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a010c926-04ec-5123-bd6e-ecc7d532f5f2.md`)

- 2026-03-04: confirmed that all 80 probes for the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot come from a newer batch with individual calibration certificates, after Altamira required no batch VS-2207 probes.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

- 2026-03-19: confirmed that the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateway supports fixed installation with mains power, for monitoring cold rooms inside stores.
- 2026-03-19: will survey five stores in Seville to plan the installation for [[Casa Verde Markets]].

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

- 2026-04-16: at the Hamburg office planning meeting, the office was set to open in HafenCity on 1 June 2026 with a 2026 budget of 420,000 euros approved by [[Hannah Brandt]]; its first hires are [[Sabine Krüger]] and [[Lukas Becker]].

(Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

- 2026-05-27: attended the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 hardware planning meeting.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/ecbc4006-43b5-542a-80a7-44fb9f384b8c.md`)

- 2026-06-01: spent the first week with the Hamburg office team after it opened.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

- 2026-07-21: at the phase 1 status meeting of the [[Casa Verde Markets]] cold-room rollout, proposed using the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 pilot units in the 9 stores where Wi-Fi does not reach the cold rooms, since v3 has LTE-M.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)
