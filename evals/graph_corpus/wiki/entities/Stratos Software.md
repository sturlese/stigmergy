# Stratos Software

## What / Who

Stratos Software is the maker of [[Stratos ERP]], the enterprise system [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl]] uses for dispatch and shipment records. (Source: `sources/2026/07/c54967da-3d89-5c8d-b202-c1056f8f92e4.md`)

Stratos ERP is an enterprise system used by food distributors in Germany and the Netherlands. [[Pieter Janssen]] is its partner manager. (Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-07-02: [[Tidewell]] is talking to it about a certified connector between the [[Frostline API]] and [[Stratos ERP]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/c54967da-3d89-5c8d-b202-c1056f8f92e4.md`)

- 2026-07-14: agreed with [[Tidewell]] that Tidewell will build a certified Stratos connector on top of the [[Frostline API]]; about 60 of its customers run refrigerated fleets, and it will list Tidewell in its partner marketplace once the connector passes certification.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)
