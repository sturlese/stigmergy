# Lotte de Vries

## What / Who

Lotte de Vries is account manager at [[Veldt Sensors]], the supplier of [[Tidewell]]'s temperature probes. (Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-02-10: represented Veldt Sensors on a call with Tidewell and confirmed that Veldt had traced drift in batch VS-2207 to a faulty resin, and that Veldt would replace every affected probe free of charge.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

- 2026-05-27: at the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 hardware planning meeting, confirmed that the resin problem behind batch VS-2207 cannot recur with the new supplier of the probe housing, and that [[Veldt Sensors]] will supply the v3 probes with an individual calibration certificate for each probe.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/ecbc4006-43b5-542a-80a7-44fb9f384b8c.md`)
