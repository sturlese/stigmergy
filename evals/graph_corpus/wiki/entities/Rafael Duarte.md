# Rafael Duarte

## What / Who

Rafael Duarte is CTO and co-founder of [[Tidewell]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-08: took ownership of passing a SOC 2 Type II audit by September, working with the security lead [[Dmitri Volkov]], because pharmaceutical customers require it.
- 2026-01-08: reported that [[Joon-ho Park]] will redesign the [[Coldpath]] dashboard in the first half of 2026, after dispatchers at [[Frostvale Distribution]] complained they cannot find the alerts that matter.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

- 2026-02-03: named owner of reviewing whether the 72-hour [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateway buffer is long enough.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/059b5629-3b54-5ba3-821e-08fcb2c7146f.md`)

- 2026-02-10: his team will add drift detection to the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] gateway firmware: when two probes in the same compartment disagree by more than 0.8 degrees for a day, [[Coldpath]] flags the pair.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

- 2026-03-12: at the [[Coldpath Redesign Review March 2026]], confirmed the excursion budget logic from [[Priya Raman]]'s memo will be live by the 20 April 2026 ship date.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/6b6348b1-877b-5da1-bc95-849a8a3c6ede.md`)

- 2026-04-16: confirmed that [[Cumulus Cloud]] has a Frankfurt region, and that the second region planned after the February outage will be Frankfurt, covering both failover and data residency.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/19f0d701-6384-5034-bf11-afa208b071d8.md`)

- 2026-05-13: at the SOC 2 Type II readiness review, confirmed the multi-region failover promised after the February outage is live in Frankfurt since 28 April.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/006a32e9-a5eb-5b14-bbfb-1a5a8b15373f.md`)

- 2026-05-27: attended the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 hardware planning meeting.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/ecbc4006-43b5-542a-80a7-44fb9f384b8c.md`)

- 2026-07-10: announced that [[Amara Nwosu]] joins [[Tidewell]] as a data engineer, reporting to him.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/b799fdea-578c-5c1a-8f76-58b921608682.md`)

- 2026-07-14: represented [[Tidewell]] on a partnership call with [[Stratos Software]] and estimated six weeks of work for a certified Stratos connector built on the [[Frostline API]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

- 2026-08-04: took part in the SOC 2 tabletop incident exercise.

(Source: `sources/2026/08/64c1d91a-92fc-55a1-8f95-cf07cb0e9a72.md`)
