# Amara Nwosu

## What / Who

Amara Nwosu is a data engineer at [[Tidewell]], reporting to CTO [[Rafael Duarte]], and works from Lisbon. She previously built grid-monitoring pipelines at [[Voltaic Grid]], an energy analytics company. (Source: `sources/2026/07/b799fdea-578c-5c1a-8f76-58b921608682.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-07-10: joined Tidewell as a data engineer, reporting to Rafael Duarte.
- 2026-07-10: her first projects are to take over the monthly excursion summaries sent to [[Halberd Mutual]], which [[Priya Raman]] built by hand; to build an analytics store of every temperature reading since 2024 so the team can study how refrigeration units fail; and to work with Priya Raman on predicting compressor failures from temperature data.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/b799fdea-578c-5c1a-8f76-58b921608682.md`)

- 2026-08-04: in [[Tidewell]]'s SOC 2 tabletop incident exercise, backups of the analytics store she built were found to be in the same region as the primary; they move to Frankfurt.

(Source: `sources/2026/08/64c1d91a-92fc-55a1-8f95-cf07cb0e9a72.md`)
