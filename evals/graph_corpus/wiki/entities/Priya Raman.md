# Priya Raman

## What / Who

Priya Raman is a staff engineer at [[Tidewell]] responsible for the alerting pipeline. (Source: `sources/2026/01/3e586328-f506-5919-af51-2773aa286315.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-22: wrote an internal memo proposing excursion budgets instead of per-crossing threshold alerts, and recommended building it before the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/3e586328-f506-5919-af51-2773aa286315.md`)

- 2026-02-03: named owner of running the alerting pipeline in two [[Cumulus Cloud]] regions with automatic failover, due end of March 2026.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/059b5629-3b54-5ba3-821e-08fcb2c7146f.md`)

- 2026-03-04: attended the kickoff of the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

- 2026-04-23: wrote an engineering design document for dwell-time alerting, specifying a dwell limit per site in [[Coldpath]] and the conditions that raise an alert.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/ce9fdf9b-593d-5421-ac1a-3685218be795.md`)

- 2026-04-30: co-wrote a [[Tidewell]] blog article on [[Alert Fatigue|alert fatigue]] in cold-chain operations with [[Joon-ho Park]].

(Source: `sources/2026/04/76cb1b34-48f6-50d9-9cc0-199d77e64b7a.md`)

- 2026-05-20: attended the review of the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot results, and will write up the Madrid door incidents with [[Dmitri Volkov]] as a blameless postmortem.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

- 2026-06-10: attended the results review of the first month of dwell-time alerting at [[Frostvale Distribution]].

(Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

- 2026-07-10: the monthly excursion summaries sent to [[Halberd Mutual]] were built by hand by her, and [[Amara Nwosu]] will take them over; Amara will also work with her on predicting compressor failures from temperature data.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/b799fdea-578c-5c1a-8f76-58b921608682.md`)

- 2026-07-17: attended the temperature-mapping workshop with [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] and estimated the automatic mapping report can be ready by the end of August.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

- 2026-08-04: took part in the SOC 2 tabletop incident exercise.

(Source: `sources/2026/08/64c1d91a-92fc-55a1-8f95-cf07cb0e9a72.md`)
