# Veldt Sensors

## What / Who

Veldt Sensors is the supplier of [[Tidewell]]'s temperature probes. [[Lotte de Vries]] is its account manager. (Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-02-10: confirmed it had traced drift in production batch VS-2207 to a faulty resin used in that batch.
- 2026-02-10: committed to replace every affected probe free of charge and cover the field engineers' travel, with replacement at [[Frostvale Distribution]] finished by 30 March.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/789e0314-ea63-54a8-b7c2-dfa80f6d595f.md`)

- 2026-03-04: its batch VS-2207 was excluded from the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot at Altamira's request, after the drift problem; the pilot's 80 probes come from a newer batch with individual calibration certificates.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

- 2026-03-28: finished replacing the 180 drifting probes from batch VS-2207 in [[Frostvale Distribution]] trucks, two days early.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

- 2026-05-13: named as one of two critical suppliers for which [[Tidewell]]'s SOC 2 readiness review found vendor risk assessments missing.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/006a32e9-a5eb-5b14-bbfb-1a5a8b15373f.md`)

- 2026-05-27: will supply the probes for the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3 with an individual calibration certificate for each probe, which pharmaceutical customers require; [[Lotte de Vries]] confirmed the resin problem behind batch VS-2207 cannot recur with the new supplier of the probe housing.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/ecbc4006-43b5-542a-80a7-44fb9f384b8c.md`)
