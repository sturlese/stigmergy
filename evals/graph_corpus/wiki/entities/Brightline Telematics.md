# Brightline Telematics

## What / Who

Brightline Telematics is a vendor that offers GPS tracking and temperature monitoring. It competed with [[Tidewell]] for [[Casa Verde Markets]]' business. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

Brightline sells GPS tracking for trucks and bundles temperature monitoring into it. Its list price is 29 euros per truck per month, which undercuts Tidewell's old flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month. (Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-03-19: had offered Casa Verde Markets a bundle of GPS tracking and temperature monitoring at a lower price per truck than Tidewell's, prompting Casa Verde to ask Tidewell to justify its higher price.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

- 2026-01: won [[Halden Seafood]] from [[Tidewell]]; [[Sigrid Aas]] said the decision was about price alone.
- 2026-03-27: a competitive note by [[Mireille Laurent]] found it alerts on every threshold crossing and has no [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]], so customers get the same alert noise [[Frostvale Distribution]] complained about; it does not produce Good Distribution Practice reports, so it cannot serve pharmaceutical distributors such as [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]]; and its temperature hardware is resold and has no per-probe calibration certificates.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

- 2026-05: two [[Halden Seafood]] salmon shipments spoiled without an alert from it; the temperature rose slowly over several hours but never crossed its threshold for long enough to trigger it, and Halden lost about 40,000 euros of product.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)
