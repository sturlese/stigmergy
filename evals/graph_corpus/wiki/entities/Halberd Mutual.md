# Halberd Mutual

## What / Who

Halberd Mutual is the cargo insurer for [[Frostvale Distribution]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

[[Oliver Pennington]] is its Head of Partnerships. (Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-15: reported to have started offering premium discounts to fleets that can prove continuous temperature monitoring.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

- 2026-03-27: [[Hannah Brandt]] is exploring a partnership with it, following [[Mireille Laurent]]'s recommendation that an insurance discount can outweigh [[Brightline Telematics]]' price difference.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

- 2026-04-02: signed a memorandum of understanding with [[Tidewell]]; it will give a 7 percent discount on cargo insurance premiums to fleets that use Tidewell with [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]] alerting switched on.
- 2026-04-02: under the memorandum, [[Tidewell]] sends it a monthly excursion summary per insured fleet — the number of excursions, minutes out of range, and whether the budget was exhausted — with no raw readings and no vehicle locations, and each customer must opt in.
- 2026-04-02: [[Oliver Pennington]] estimated that the first joint customer, [[Frostvale Distribution]], would save about 48,000 euros a year.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/067f41cc-472f-5ede-8b6c-dfeaa680e01e.md`)

- 2026-04-09: [[Frostvale Distribution]] opted in to its discount programme.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

- 2026-06-10: confirmed that [[Frostvale Distribution]] is eligible for the 7 percent premium discount from July, based on the first monthly excursion summaries.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/c53e64d7-7552-5fe9-a458-e2f48f6820b8.md`)

- 2026-06-17: [[Halden Seafood]], shopping for a new cargo insurer after spoiled shipments, asked about its discount programme.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

- 2026-07-07: started onboarding [[Halden Seafood]] as a new cargo insurance customer; [[Oliver Pennington]] confirmed Halden becomes eligible for the 7 percent premium discount after two months of excursion summaries.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)
