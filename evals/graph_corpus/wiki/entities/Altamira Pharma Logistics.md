# Altamira Pharma Logistics

## What / Who

Altamira Pharma Logistics is a prospective customer that [[Tidewell]] is targeting to enter pharmaceutical distribution. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

Altamira distributes vaccines and biologics in Portugal and Spain. (Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-08: named as the target of a pilot that [[Mireille Laurent]] owns, intended to bring Tidewell into pharmaceutical distribution.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

- 2026-01-22: an internal memo recommended building excursion budgets before its pilot, because pharmaceutical customers already think in excursion budgets.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/3e586328-f506-5919-af51-2773aa286315.md`)

- 2026-03-04: kicked off a 90-day pilot with [[Tidewell]] covering 40 delivery vans based in Porto and Madrid, for products that must stay between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.
- 2026-03-04: its Quality Director [[Kwame Mensah]] set three success criteria: zero excursions Tidewell fails to detect, reports its quality team can file for Good Distribution Practice audits without rework, and alerts based on an excursion budget of 60 cumulative minutes per shipment.
- 2026-03-04: required that no [[Veldt Sensors]] batch VS-2207 probe be used in the pilot; all 80 pilot probes come from a newer batch with individual calibration certificates.
- 2026-03-04: agreed a pilot fee of 18,000 euros; a production contract would follow only if the pilot succeeds, and its procurement will require a SOC 2 Type II report first.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

- 2026-05-13: will not sign a production contract without the SOC 2 report.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/006a32e9-a5eb-5b14-bbfb-1a5a8b15373f.md`)

- 2026-05-20: completed the 90-day pilot with [[Tidewell]]: 11,800 trips monitored, 23 temperature excursions detected, 21 within the 60-minute [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]] and 2 that exhausted it, both caused by a van door left open at the Madrid depot; Altamira checked the results against its own data loggers and found zero excursions Tidewell missed.
- 2026-05-20: its quality team filed Tidewell's Good Distribution Practice reports in an internal audit without rework, which [[Kwame Mensah]] called the most important result.
- 2026-05-20: decided to move to a production contract covering 310 vans across Portugal and Spain from September 2026, worth about 190,000 euros of annual recurring revenue, conditional on Tidewell delivering its SOC 2 Type II report.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

- 2026-07-17: at a temperature-mapping workshop with [[Tidewell]], its compliance officer [[Elena Marín]] explained that Good Distribution Practice expects temperature mapping of every vehicle type, repeated each summer and winter, and that Altamira currently runs mapping studies with a consultant and paper reports, taking about two weeks per van type.
- 2026-07-17: agreed to a proposal for Tidewell to generate mapping reports automatically from vans fitted with extra probes during a mapping week, showing the temperature distribution by position and the warmest location; [[Elena Marín]] will send the report format its inspectors accept.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dd0f247e-5dbb-57cd-854c-419824760f67.md`)

- 2026-07-28: represented at the first session of Tidewell's customer advisory board by [[Kwame Mensah]], who ranked automatic temperature mapping reports third and called them essential for pharma.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
