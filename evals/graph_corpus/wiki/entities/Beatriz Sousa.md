# Beatriz Sousa

## What / Who

Beatriz Sousa is Head of Fresh at [[Casa Verde Markets]], a grocery chain that uses [[Tidewell]] on its delivery trucks in Portugal. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-03-19: at an account meeting, said Casa Verde is opening 65 stores in Andalusia this year and wants to monitor the cold rooms inside the stores as well as the trucks, about 1,300 cold rooms across the new and existing stores.
- 2026-03-19: raised a concern that [[Brightline Telematics]] has offered Casa Verde a bundle of GPS tracking and temperature monitoring at a lower price per truck, and asked Tidewell to explain why it is worth more.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

- 2026-07-21: at the phase 1 status meeting of the cold-room rollout, was satisfied with the pace and asked that phase 2, another 900 cold rooms, start in October.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)

- 2026-07-28: at the first session of Tidewell's customer advisory board, ranked multi-temperature compartments second, needed for Casa Verde's mixed deliveries.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
