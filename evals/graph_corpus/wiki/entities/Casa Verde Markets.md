# Casa Verde Markets

## What / Who

Casa Verde Markets is a customer of [[Tidewell]], affected by the [[Cumulus Cloud]] outage of 1 February 2026. (Source: `sources/2026/02/059b5629-3b54-5ba3-821e-08fcb2c7146f.md`)

Casa Verde Markets is a grocery chain that uses [[Tidewell]] on its delivery trucks in Portugal. (Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-02-01: was among 14 Tidewell customers affected by the Cumulus Cloud eu-west outage; two of its trucks had temperature excursions during the outage that were only reported afterwards, and the product was inspected and kept.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/059b5629-3b54-5ba3-821e-08fcb2c7146f.md`)

- 2026-02-26: modelled to pay about 5 percent less under per-asset pricing, because its trucks are simpler.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

- 2026-03-12: three of its dispatchers took part in a usability test of the redesigned [[Coldpath]] home screen.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/6b6348b1-877b-5da1-bc95-849a8a3c6ede.md`)

- 2026-03-19: is opening 65 stores in Andalusia in 2026 and wants to monitor the cold rooms inside the stores as well as its trucks, about 1,300 cold rooms across the new and existing stores.
- 2026-03-19: under per-asset pricing, a deal covering those cold rooms would be worth about 260,000 euros of annual recurring revenue, which would make it Tidewell's second-largest customer.
- 2026-03-19: [[Beatriz Sousa]] raised that [[Brightline Telematics]] had offered a bundle of GPS tracking and temperature monitoring at a lower price per truck.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

- 2026-05-27: the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v3's LTE-M connectivity with Wi-Fi fallback is needed for its in-store cold room rollout.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/ecbc4006-43b5-542a-80a7-44fb9f384b8c.md`)

- 2026-06-30: signed the Spanish expansion, including cold rooms in 65 new stores, per the first-half review.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/f0965a0c-1c57-529c-a4f0-679d12f235f1.md`)

- 2026-07-14: runs [[Stratos ERP]] in its Spanish warehouses and will follow [[Nordhafen Tiefkühl]] as a joint customer of the certified connector between [[Tidewell]] and [[Stratos Software]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

- 2026-07-21: phase 1 of its cold-room rollout covers 400 cold rooms in 30 stores in Andalusia, with 180 cold rooms live in 14 stores; [[Refritec Instalações]] installs the probes and mounts the gateways and [[Tidewell]] configures the sites in [[Coldpath]].
- 2026-07-21: in 9 stores the cold rooms sit behind thick walls where Wi-Fi does not reach, and the [[Tidewell Sentinel]] v2 gateway has no cellular fallback for fixed installations; [[Tomasz Zielinski]] proposed using the Sentinel v3 pilot units there.
- 2026-07-21: [[Beatriz Sousa]] asked that phase 2, another 900 cold rooms, start in October.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/dab77dff-5866-5868-8074-3fa9683636ba.md`)

- 2026-07-28: represented at the first session of Tidewell's customer advisory board by [[Beatriz Sousa]], who ranked multi-temperature compartments second for its mixed deliveries.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
