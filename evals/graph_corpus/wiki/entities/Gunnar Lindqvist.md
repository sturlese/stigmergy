# Gunnar Lindqvist

## What / Who

Gunnar Lindqvist is VP Logistics at [[Frostvale Distribution]], a customer of [[Tidewell]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-15: represented Frostvale at its quarterly business review with Tidewell, where he raised alert noise as his main complaint and said his team has started ignoring [[Coldpath]] notifications.
- 2026-01-15: requested dwell-time awareness and a single view per trip, and said Frostvale will not renew on current terms unless the alert noise drops.
- 2026-01-15: asked whether Tidewell reports could serve as proof of continuous temperature monitoring for a premium discount from [[Halberd Mutual]].

(Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

- 2026-06-03: gave [[Sabine Krüger]] permission to use Frostvale's results on alert noise as references for German prospects.
