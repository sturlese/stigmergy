# Mireille Laurent

## What / Who

Mireille Laurent is Head of Sales at [[Tidewell]]. (Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-01-08: took ownership of signing a pilot with [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] to enter pharmaceutical distribution.
- 2026-01-08: with the incoming CFO, took ownership of designing a replacement for the flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month, moving to pricing per monitored asset.

(Source: `sources/2026/01/60e59b80-2e07-52cb-9a03-a4b3d40e34fd.md`)

- 2026-01-15: represented [[Tidewell]] at [[Frostvale Distribution]]'s quarterly business review alongside [[Aurélie Fontaine]].

(Source: `sources/2026/01/5649a227-256b-5fc1-9ddd-551c0b8b3de4.md`)

- 2026-02-26: at the pricing workshop, agreed to pitch per-asset pricing as land-and-expand, so a customer can start by monitoring a few trucks and grow into cold rooms and more sensors without renegotiating the contract.

(Source: `sources/2026/02/a72c62aa-a32d-5e01-8437-bdc6a7a85b1a.md`)

- 2026-03-04: attended the kickoff of the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot with [[Kwame Mensah]].

(Source: `sources/2026/03/7fea1cb0-76c8-5fee-bf88-e5d11f072207.md`)

- 2026-03-19: at an account meeting with [[Casa Verde Markets]], took on sending the customer a proposal by 2 April.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/50f70755-f5c4-5148-a444-1e4187125b02.md`)

- 2026-03-27: wrote a competitive note for the sales team about [[Brightline Telematics]]; recommends leading with compliance and the cost of spoiled cargo rather than price per truck, and a partnership with an insurer because an insurance discount can outweigh the price difference.

(Source: `sources/2026/03/62b65f0b-56b9-5b32-8640-0cf17bda9932.md`)

- 2026-04-09: represented Tidewell at the [[Frostvale Distribution]] renewal meeting.

(Source: `sources/2026/04/af1eefa5-0082-5c2c-92bc-a108b57dcce0.md`)

- 2026-05-20: attended the review of the [[Altamira Pharma Logistics]] pilot results.

(Source: `sources/2026/05/764e9f2e-7356-53ba-8931-f52a87c758b2.md`)

- 2026-06-17: took a win-back call from [[Sigrid Aas]] of [[Halden Seafood]]; explained that a slow temperature rise consumes the [[Excursion Budget|excursion budget]] and would have raised an alert at 50 percent, and will send terms by 24 June for a return with 60 trucks on [[Per-Asset Pricing|per-asset pricing]] and a one-month pilot in July.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/b7b68a5b-c333-5c46-8831-2006543cfc9b.md`)

- 2026-07-07: represented [[Tidewell]] at the start of [[Halden Seafood]]'s one-month pilot.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e64e610b-8873-5f99-b8ae-72f819367db6.md`)
