# Nordhafen Tiefkühl

## What / Who

Nordhafen Tiefkühl is a frozen-food distributor in northern Germany with 150 trucks. It is a prospect of [[Tidewell]]'s Hamburg office. (Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

Nordhafen Tiefkühl is based in Bremen. Its Head of Fleet is [[Klaus Ehrlich]] and its IT lead is [[Maren Vogt]]. (Source: `sources/2026/07/c54967da-3d89-5c8d-b202-c1056f8f92e4.md`)

## Facts

- 2026-06-03: reported as the most advanced of 9 German frozen-food distributors in conversation with Tidewell's Hamburg office, and wants a pilot in September.

(Source: `sources/2026/06/98690a71-c5fc-5cfe-8b9c-2dde0530ed7d.md`)

- 2026-07-02: kicked off a pilot with [[Tidewell]] covering 30 trucks on the Bremen–Hamburg routes from 1 September 2026 for 60 days, with a pilot fee of 9,000 euros.
- 2026-07-02: [[Maren Vogt]] required that Tidewell alerts appear inside [[Stratos ERP]], its enterprise system for dispatch and shipment records; [[Klaus Ehrlich]] asked for German-language reports and for the data to stay in the Frankfurt region of [[Cumulus Cloud]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/c54967da-3d89-5c8d-b202-c1056f8f92e4.md`)

- 2026-07-14: named the first joint customer of the certified connector between [[Tidewell]] and [[Stratos Software]].

(Source: `sources/2026/07/e17d5404-01e2-535c-8c1a-a03bf0d45a03.md`)

- 2026-07-28: represented at the first session of Tidewell's customer advisory board by [[Klaus Ehrlich]], who asked that the board meet twice a year and that members see the roadmap before other customers.

(Source: `sources/2026/07/79091125-3d38-52e3-91b3-ad372c20a374.md`)
