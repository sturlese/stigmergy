### Readable content

Call on 17 June 2026 between Sigrid Aas, operations manager at Halden Seafood, and Mireille Laurent.

Halden Seafood left Tidewell for Brightline Telematics in January 2026 because of price. Sigrid called because two salmon shipments spoiled in May without any alert: the temperature rose slowly over several hours but never crossed Brightline's threshold for long enough to trigger it. Halden lost about 40,000 euros of product.

Sigrid had read about excursion budgets and asked whether Tidewell would have caught those shipments. Mireille explained that a slow rise consumes the excursion budget and would have raised an alert at 50 percent.

Sigrid also asked about the Halberd Mutual discount, since Halden is shopping for a new cargo insurer after the claims.

Proposal: Halden returns with 60 trucks on per-asset pricing, starting with a one-month pilot in July. Mireille will send terms by 24 June.
