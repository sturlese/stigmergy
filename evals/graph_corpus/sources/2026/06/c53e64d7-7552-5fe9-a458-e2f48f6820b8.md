### Readable content

Results review on 10 June 2026 with Gunnar Lindqvist of Frostvale Distribution, Aurélie Fontaine and Priya Raman.

Dwell-time alerting went live for Frostvale on 15 May 2026, as committed in the renewal.

Results for the first full month:
- alerts fell from about 1,900 to 930 a month, a 51 percent reduction, better than the 45 percent predicted in the design;
- the acknowledged-alert ratio rose from 22 percent to 64 percent;
- no excursion that exhausted its budget went unalerted.

Gunnar said his dispatchers now trust Coldpath again. He still wants the ratio above 70 percent and asked for quiet hours at the two cross-docking hubs in Setúbal and Zaragoza.

Halberd Mutual confirmed that Frostvale is eligible for the 7 percent premium discount from July, based on the first monthly excursion summaries.
