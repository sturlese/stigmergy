### Readable content

Quarterly business review with Frostvale Distribution on 15 January 2026. From Frostvale: Gunnar Lindqvist, VP Logistics. From Tidewell: Aurélie Fontaine (customer success manager for the account) and Mireille Laurent.

Frostvale runs 420 refrigerated trucks, all fitted with Tidewell Sentinel gateways, delivering frozen food to supermarkets in Portugal and Spain.

Gunnar's main complaint was alert noise. In December his dispatchers received 1,900 temperature alerts; 70 percent resolved on their own within ten minutes, usually because a door was opened during a planned delivery. Dispatchers acknowledged only 22 percent of alerts. Gunnar said his team has started ignoring Coldpath notifications altogether.

He asked for two things: alerts that understand planned stops at loading docks (he called it "dwell-time awareness"), and a single view per trip instead of a flat list of alerts.

The Frostvale contract renews in April 2026. Gunnar will not renew on the current terms unless the alert noise drops.

Gunnar also mentioned that their cargo insurer, Halberd Mutual, has started offering premium discounts to fleets that can prove continuous temperature monitoring. He asked whether Tidewell reports could serve as that proof. Aurélie will follow up.
