### Readable content

Notes from the Tidewell leadership kickoff held on 8 January 2026 in the Lisbon office. Present: Ines Okafor (CEO and co-founder), Rafael Duarte (CTO and co-founder), Mireille Laurent (Head of Sales) and Tomasz Zielinski (Head of Operations).

Tidewell sells cold-chain monitoring for refrigerated transport: the Tidewell Sentinel gateway sits in each truck, reads temperature probes and door sensors, and streams readings to Coldpath, the web dashboard dispatchers use. Customers can also pull data through the Frostline API.

Ines opened with the numbers: annual recurring revenue closed 2025 at 2.1 million euros across 31 customers. Frostvale Distribution alone is 38 percent of ARR, which the team agreed is a concentration risk.

The four priorities agreed for 2026:

1. Sign a pilot with Altamira Pharma Logistics to enter pharmaceutical distribution. Mireille owns it.
2. Open a Hamburg office by June to sell to German frozen-food distributors. Tomasz owns the setup.
3. Pass a SOC 2 Type II audit by September, because pharma customers require it. Rafael owns it with the security lead, Dmitri Volkov.
4. Replace the flat fee of 39 euros per truck per month with pricing per monitored asset. Mireille and the incoming CFO own the design.

Rafael added that Joon-ho Park will redesign the Coldpath dashboard in the first half, because dispatchers at Frostvale complain that they cannot find the alerts that matter.
