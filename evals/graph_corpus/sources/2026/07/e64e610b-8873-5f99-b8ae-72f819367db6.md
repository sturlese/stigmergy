Halden Seafood started its one-month pilot with Tidewell on 7 July 2026, after the win-back call in June. Halden: Sigrid Aas, operations manager, and Tiago Ferreira, quality assurance manager. Tidewell: Mireille Laurent and Aurélie Fontaine.

The pilot covers 60 trucks carrying fresh salmon from the port of Aveiro to supermarkets in Portugal. Excursion budgets are set at 20 cumulative minutes above 4 degrees Celsius, the limit Tiago's quality team uses for fresh fish.

Halden also started onboarding with Halberd Mutual as its new cargo insurer. Oliver Pennington joined the call and confirmed that Halden becomes eligible for the 7 percent premium discount after two months of excursion summaries.

Aurélie trained Halden's four dispatchers on the Coldpath trip view.
