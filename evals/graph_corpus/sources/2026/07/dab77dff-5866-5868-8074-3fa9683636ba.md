### Readable content

Status meeting on 21 July 2026. Casa Verde Markets: Beatriz Sousa. Tidewell: Tomasz Zielinski. Installer: Nuno Carvalho of Refritec Instalações, the refrigeration contractor Casa Verde uses in its stores.

Phase 1 covers 400 cold rooms in 30 stores in Andalusia. Refritec installs the probes and mounts the gateways; Tidewell configures the sites in Coldpath.

Progress: 180 cold rooms live in 14 stores.

Problem: in 9 stores the cold rooms sit behind thick walls where Wi-Fi does not reach, and the current Sentinel v2 gateway has no cellular fallback for fixed installations. Tomasz proposed using the Sentinel v3 pilot units in those stores, since v3 has LTE-M.

Beatriz is satisfied with the pace and asked that phase 2, another 900 cold rooms, start in October.
