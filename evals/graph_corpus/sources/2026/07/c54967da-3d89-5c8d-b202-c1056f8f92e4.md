### Readable content

Kickoff on 2 July 2026 for the pilot with Nordhafen Tiefkühl, a frozen-food distributor based in Bremen with 150 trucks. Nordhafen: Klaus Ehrlich, Head of Fleet, and Maren Vogt, IT lead. Tidewell: Sabine Krüger and Lukas Becker.

Scope: 30 trucks on the Bremen–Hamburg routes from 1 September 2026 for 60 days, with a pilot fee of 9,000 euros.

Maren requires that Tidewell alerts appear inside Stratos ERP, the enterprise system Nordhafen uses for dispatch and shipment records. Sabine confirmed that the Frostline API can push excursion events to Stratos, and that Tidewell is talking to Stratos Software about a certified connector.

Klaus asked for German-language reports and for the data to stay in the Frankfurt region of Cumulus Cloud. Both were confirmed.

Lukas will install the gateways at the Bremen depot in the last week of August.
