### Readable content

Partnership call on 14 July 2026 between Tidewell and Stratos Software, maker of Stratos ERP, an enterprise system used by food distributors in Germany and the Netherlands. Stratos: Pieter Janssen, partner manager. Tidewell: Sabine Krüger and Rafael Duarte.

Agreement: Tidewell will build a certified Stratos connector on top of the Frostline API. Excursion events and GDP reports will attach to the matching shipment record in Stratos ERP.

Pieter said about 60 Stratos customers run refrigerated fleets, and Stratos will list Tidewell in its partner marketplace once the connector passes certification.

The first joint customer is Nordhafen Tiefkühl. Casa Verde Markets, which also runs Stratos ERP in its Spanish warehouses, will follow.

Rafael estimates six weeks of work for the connector.
