### Readable content

First session of the Tidewell customer advisory board, 28 July 2026, hosted by Ines Okafor. Members: Gunnar Lindqvist (Frostvale Distribution), Beatriz Sousa (Casa Verde Markets), Dr. Kwame Mensah (Altamira Pharma Logistics) and Klaus Ehrlich (Nordhafen Tiefkühl).

Each member ranked the roadmap:
1. Predicting refrigeration failures before they happen. Gunnar said a failed compressor costs Frostvale about 25,000 euros of product per incident.
2. Multi-temperature compartments, for trucks that carry frozen and chilled goods at once. Beatriz needs this for Casa Verde's mixed deliveries.
3. Automatic temperature mapping reports, which Dr. Mensah called essential for pharma.
4. A mobile app for drivers.

Klaus asked that the board meet twice a year and that members see the roadmap before other customers.
