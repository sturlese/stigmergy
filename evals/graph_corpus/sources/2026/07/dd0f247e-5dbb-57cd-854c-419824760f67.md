### Readable content

Workshop on 17 July 2026 with Altamira Pharma Logistics. Altamira: Dr. Kwame Mensah and Elena Marín, compliance officer. Tidewell: Priya Raman and Joon-ho Park.

Elena explained that Good Distribution Practice expects temperature mapping of every vehicle type: a study that places several probes across the cargo space to find hot and cold spots, repeated each summer and winter.

Today Altamira runs mapping studies with a consultant and paper reports, which takes about two weeks per van type.

Proposal: Tidewell generates mapping reports automatically from vans fitted with extra probes during a mapping week, showing the temperature distribution by position and the warmest location.

Elena will send the report format that Altamira's inspectors accept. Joon-ho will design the report; Priya estimates it can be ready by the end of August.
