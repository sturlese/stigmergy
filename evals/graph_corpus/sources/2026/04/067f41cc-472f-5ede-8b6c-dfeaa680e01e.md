### Readable content

Summary of the memorandum of understanding signed on 2 April 2026 between Tidewell and Halberd Mutual, a cargo insurer. Halberd Mutual: Oliver Pennington, Head of Partnerships. Tidewell: Ines Okafor and Hannah Brandt.

Halberd Mutual will give a 7 percent discount on cargo insurance premiums to fleets that use Tidewell with excursion budget alerting switched on.

Data sharing is deliberately narrow: each month Tidewell sends Halberd an excursion summary per insured fleet (number of excursions, minutes out of range, budget exhausted or not). No raw readings and no vehicle locations are shared. Each customer must opt in.

The first joint customer will be Frostvale Distribution, which already insures its cargo with Halberd. Oliver estimated Frostvale's saving at about 48,000 euros a year.

Hannah sees the partnership as the answer to Brightline Telematics in price-driven deals: the insurance saving is larger than the price gap per truck.
