### Readable content

Engineering design document by Priya Raman, 23 April 2026.

Problem: most alerts at Frostvale Distribution come from planned stops. When a truck is parked at a loading dock with its doors open, temperature rises and threshold alerts fire, although nothing is wrong.

Design: every site in Coldpath gets a dwell limit, the time a vehicle may stay at that site with doors open, for example 25 minutes at a supermarket dock. While a vehicle is within its dwell limit at a known site, temperature crossings count against the excursion budget but raise no alert. An alert fires only when the dwell limit is exceeded, when the door stays open away from a known site, or when the excursion budget reaches 80 percent.

The Sentinel gateway already reports door sensor state and GPS position, so no hardware change is needed.

Expected result: alert volume at Frostvale falls by about 45 percent without missing any excursion that consumes the budget.

Rollout: all customers on 15 May 2026, starting with Frostvale as promised in its renewal.
