Pilot review on 20 May 2026 with Dr. Kwame Mensah of Altamira Pharma Logistics, Mireille Laurent and Priya Raman.

Results of the 90-day pilot with 40 vans in Porto and Madrid:
- 11,800 trips monitored;
- 23 temperature excursions detected;
- 21 excursions stayed within the 60-minute excursion budget; 2 exhausted it, both caused by a van door left open at the Madrid depot;
- zero excursions missed, checked by Altamira against its own data loggers.

Altamira's quality team filed Tidewell's Good Distribution Practice reports in an internal audit without rework, which Dr. Mensah called the most important result.

Decision: Altamira will move to a production contract covering 310 vans across Portugal and Spain from September 2026, worth about 190,000 euros of annual recurring revenue. The contract is conditional on Tidewell delivering its SOC 2 Type II report.

Priya will write up the Madrid door incidents with Dmitri Volkov as a blameless postmortem.
