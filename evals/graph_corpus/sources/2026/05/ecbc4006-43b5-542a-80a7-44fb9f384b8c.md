### Readable content

Hardware planning meeting on 27 May 2026 with Rafael Duarte, Tomasz Zielinski and Lotte de Vries from Veldt Sensors.

Tidewell Sentinel v3 will replace the current v2 gateway. Changes:
- LTE-M connectivity with Wi-Fi fallback for cold rooms in stores, needed for the Casa Verde Markets rollout;
- a local buffer of 7 days instead of 72 hours, following the review after the Cumulus Cloud outage;
- two probe inputs per compartment, so drift detection can compare paired probes in firmware.

Veldt Sensors will supply the probes for v3 with an individual calibration certificate for each probe, which pharmaceutical customers require. Lotte confirmed that the resin problem behind batch VS-2207 cannot recur with the new supplier of the probe housing.

Unit cost falls from 97 euros for v2 to 84 euros for v3.

Timeline: pilot units in August, production from October 2026. Existing v2 gateways stay supported until 2028.
