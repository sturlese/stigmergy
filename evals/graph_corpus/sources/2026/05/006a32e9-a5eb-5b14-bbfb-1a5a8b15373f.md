### Readable content

Readiness review on 13 May 2026. Present: Dmitri Volkov (security and reliability lead), Rafael Duarte, and two auditors from Carrow & Finch, the audit firm Tidewell engaged.

The SOC 2 Type II observation window runs from 1 May to 31 August 2026, so the report can be issued in late September.

Gaps found:
- Access reviews for production systems are not documented. Dmitri will run them every quarter.
- Vendor risk assessments are missing for the two critical suppliers, Cumulus Cloud and Veldt Sensors.
- Incident response has never been tested. The auditors accepted the blameless postmortem of the February Cumulus Cloud outage as evidence of the process, but asked for a tabletop exercise before August.

Why it matters: Altamira Pharma Logistics will not sign a production contract without the SOC 2 report, and German distributors contacted by the Hamburg team ask for it as well.

Rafael confirmed that the multi-region failover promised after the outage is live in Frankfurt since 28 April.
