### Readable content

Kickoff on 4 March 2026 for the pilot with Altamira Pharma Logistics, which distributes vaccines and biologics in Portugal and Spain. Altamira: Dr. Kwame Mensah, Quality Director. Tidewell: Mireille Laurent, Tomasz Zielinski and Priya Raman.

Scope: 40 delivery vans based in Porto and Madrid for 90 days. Products must stay between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.

Success criteria set by Dr. Mensah:
- zero excursions that Tidewell fails to detect;
- reports that Altamira's quality team can file for Good Distribution Practice audits without rework;
- alerts based on an excursion budget of 60 cumulative minutes per shipment, matching Altamira's own procedures.

Dr. Mensah required that no probe from Veldt Sensors batch VS-2207 be used in the pilot, after hearing about the drift problem. Tomasz confirmed that all 80 pilot probes come from a newer batch with individual calibration certificates.

Commercial terms: a pilot fee of 18,000 euros. If the pilot succeeds, Altamira will discuss a production contract, but Dr. Mensah said their procurement will require a SOC 2 Type II report first.
