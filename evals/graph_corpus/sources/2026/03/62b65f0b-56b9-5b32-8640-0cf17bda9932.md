Competitive note written by Mireille Laurent for the sales team, 27 March 2026.

Brightline Telematics sells GPS tracking for trucks and bundles temperature monitoring into it. Their list price is 29 euros per truck per month, which undercuts Tidewell's old flat fee of 39 euros.

Where Brightline is weaker:
- It alerts on every threshold crossing and has no excursion budget, so customers get the same alert noise that Frostvale Distribution complained about.
- It does not produce Good Distribution Practice reports, so it cannot serve pharmaceutical distributors such as Altamira Pharma Logistics.
- Its temperature hardware is resold and has no per-probe calibration certificates.

Brightline won one Tidewell customer: Halden Seafood switched to Brightline in January 2026. Sigrid Aas, Halden's operations manager, said the decision was about price alone.

How to respond: lead with compliance and with the cost of spoiled cargo, not with price per truck. Mireille also recommends a partnership with an insurer, because an insurance discount can outweigh the price difference. Hannah Brandt is exploring this with Halberd Mutual.
