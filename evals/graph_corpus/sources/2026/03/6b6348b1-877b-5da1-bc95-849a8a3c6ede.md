### Readable content

Design review on 12 March 2026. Present: Joon-ho Park (product designer), Rafael Duarte and Aurélie Fontaine.

Joon-ho presented the redesigned Coldpath home screen:
- an excursion budget gauge for every active shipment, turning amber at 50 percent and red at 80 percent;
- an alert inbox grouped by trip, so a dispatcher sees one row per truck journey instead of dozens of separate alerts;
- quiet hours per site, during which planned loading does not raise alerts.

Joon-ho ran a usability test with six dispatchers, three from Frostvale Distribution and three from Casa Verde Markets. The time to find the one shipment that needed action dropped from 4 minutes 10 seconds on the old screen to 1 minute 50 seconds on the new one. Five of six dispatchers preferred the trip grouping.

Aurélie asked for a weekly email summary for customers who do not open Coldpath every day. Joon-ho added it to the backlog.

Decision: the redesign ships on 20 April 2026. Rafael confirmed that the excursion budget logic from Priya Raman's memo will be live by then.
