Account meeting on 19 March 2026 with Casa Verde Markets, a grocery chain that uses Tidewell on its delivery trucks in Portugal. Casa Verde: Beatriz Sousa, Head of Fresh. Tidewell: Mireille Laurent and Tomasz Zielinski.

Casa Verde is opening 65 stores in Andalusia this year. Beatriz wants to monitor the cold rooms inside the stores as well as the trucks: about 1,300 cold rooms across the new and existing stores. Tomasz confirmed that the Tidewell Sentinel gateway supports fixed installation with mains power.

With per-asset pricing, the deal would be worth about 260,000 euros of annual recurring revenue, which would make Casa Verde Tidewell's second-largest customer.

Beatriz raised a concern: Brightline Telematics has offered Casa Verde a bundle of GPS tracking and temperature monitoring at a lower price per truck. She asked Tidewell to explain why it is worth more.

Next steps: Mireille will send a proposal by 2 April. Tomasz will survey five stores in Seville to plan the installation.
