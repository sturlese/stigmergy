### Readable content

Workshop on 26 February 2026 with Mireille Laurent, Hannah Brandt and Ines Okafor.

Today every customer pays a flat 39 euros per truck per month, whatever the truck carries. A truck with one probe pays the same as a truck with six probes and two door sensors, and fixed cold rooms in stores cannot be priced at all.

Proposal agreed: price per monitored asset. Each probe or door sensor costs 14 euros per month and each Sentinel gateway 9 euros per month.

Hannah modelled the effect on current customers:
- Frostvale Distribution, whose trucks carry several probes each, would pay about 12 percent more.
- Casa Verde Markets, with simpler trucks, would pay about 5 percent less.

Decisions:
- New customers get per-asset pricing from 1 April 2026.
- Existing customers move to it at their next renewal, with the increase capped at 10 percent in the first year.
- Mireille will pitch it as land-and-expand: a customer can start by monitoring a few trucks and grow into cold rooms and more sensors without renegotiating the contract.
