Internal announcement from Ines Okafor, 18 February 2026.

Hannah Brandt joins Tidewell today as Chief Financial Officer and reports to Ines. Hannah was previously finance director at Marlow Freight, a logistics scale-up, where she led the move from per-vehicle to usage-based pricing and ran two funding rounds.

At Tidewell, Hannah will own three things in her first six months:
- the financial model for the new pricing per monitored asset, together with Mireille Laurent;
- the budget for the Hamburg office that Tomasz Zielinski is setting up;
- preparation for the Series A round, working with the existing investor Kittiwake Capital.

Please welcome her. She sits with the commercial team in Lisbon on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
