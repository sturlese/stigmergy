### Readable content

Call on 10 February 2026 between Tidewell and Veldt Sensors, the supplier of Tidewell's temperature probes. Veldt: Lotte de Vries, account manager. Tidewell: Tomasz Zielinski and Rafael Duarte.

Tomasz reported that probes from production batch VS-2207 read about 1.4 degrees Celsius too warm after roughly nine months in service. Field engineers found it when comparing probes against reference thermometers at Frostvale Distribution.

About 600 probes from that batch are installed across Tidewell customers, 180 of them in Frostvale trucks.

Lotte confirmed that Veldt had traced the drift to a faulty resin used in that batch. Veldt will replace every affected probe free of charge and cover the field engineers' travel. Replacement at Frostvale will be finished by 30 March.

Decisions:
- Tidewell will run a calibration audit every quarter, comparing a sample of probes against reference thermometers.
- Rafael's team will add drift detection to the Sentinel gateway firmware: when two probes in the same compartment disagree by more than 0.8 degrees for a day, Coldpath flags the pair.
- Tomasz will keep a register of probe batches per customer so that a future defect can be traced in hours instead of weeks.
