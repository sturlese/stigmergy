### Readable content

Blameless postmortem written by Dmitri Volkov, security and reliability lead, on 3 February 2026.

What happened: on 1 February 2026 the eu-west region of Cumulus Cloud, Tidewell's hosting provider, was unavailable for 3 hours and 40 minutes. The Tidewell ingestion service and the alerting pipeline both ran only in that region, so no alerts were sent during the outage.

Impact: 14 customers were affected, including Casa Verde Markets and Frostvale Distribution. No readings were lost, because every Tidewell Sentinel gateway buffers up to 72 hours of readings locally and uploaded them when the region came back. Two Casa Verde trucks had temperature excursions during the outage that were only reported afterwards; the product was inspected and kept.

What went well: the local buffer in the Sentinel gateway worked as designed. Customer success, led by Aurélie Fontaine, called every affected customer within two hours.

What went wrong: a single hosting region for the alerting pipeline, and no public status page.

Actions:
- Run the alerting pipeline in two Cumulus Cloud regions with automatic failover. Owner: Priya Raman. Due: end of March.
- Publish a status page. Owner: Dmitri Volkov. Due: end of February.
- Review whether the 72-hour gateway buffer is long enough. Owner: Rafael Duarte.
