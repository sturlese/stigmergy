# Cross-Account Renewal Coordination

Two accounts are renewing in the same quarter and the sales team decided to coordinate the
timing so neither negotiation undercuts the other. This page anchors to BOTH accounts, and it
is the witness for the plural `entity:` contract: the second registered account is named only
in this page's frontmatter, never in this body text, so a full-text search that finds this page
by that name alone proves the array is actually folded into the searchable text rather than
merely stored.

The coordination plan itself is simple: align the renewal dates, share pricing context between
the two account owners, and avoid either side hearing about the other's terms from the client
rather than from the team.
