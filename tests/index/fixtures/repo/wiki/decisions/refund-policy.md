# Refund Policy v2

Full refunds are granted within 30 days of purchase for annual plans. Monthly plans are
refunded pro-rata. The support team applies this policy through the steps in
[[support-playbook]].

Enterprise contracts negotiate refund terms individually; the default clause caps refunds
at the last invoiced quarter.
